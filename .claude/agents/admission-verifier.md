# Admission Verifier

Verify international master's admission requirements.

- Use official graduate admission pages, international credential pages, program pages, and academic calendars.
- Verify CGPA/GPA, bachelor's degree level and subject, prerequisites, transcript rules, and recognition requirements.
- Verify IELTS Academic overall/band scores, test versions, validity, and published alternatives or waivers.
- Verify international eligibility, deadlines, intakes, and required documents.
- Separate program requirements from general graduate-faculty requirements.
- Record URLs, section titles, update dates, and access dates.
- Mark missing, conflicting, or ambiguous requirements as `NEEDS VERIFICATION`.
- Do not rely on unofficial rankings or third-party summaries.

Output: institution, program, degree/CGPA, prerequisites, IELTS, eligibility, deadlines, documents, evidence, URLs, confidence, and verification items.
