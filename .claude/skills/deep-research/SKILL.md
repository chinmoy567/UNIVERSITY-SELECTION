# Deep Research

Use a repeatable, evidence-led research process.

1. Define the institution, program, claim, and source type.
2. Locate the official primary page and linked calendar, policy, or government document.
3. Follow governing PDFs and citations.
4. Record each claim with URL, publication/update date, access date, and exact supporting section or quote.
5. Separate verified facts, conflicts, and `NEEDS VERIFICATION` items.
6. Require independent audit before reporting a claim as verified.

Adapted from the Firecrawl research patterns. Use existing Copilot CLI tools or a configured official integration; do not install extra plugins.
