# Source Verification

Every important claim must be traceable to primary evidence.

- Reopen the cited URL and inspect the current page or document.
- Prefer official university, faculty, graduate faculty, academic calendar, IRCC, provincial, and territorial government sources.
- Record source owner, title, URL, publication/update date, access date, and exact evidence.
- Classify `VERIFIED`, `CONFLICTING`, or `NEEDS VERIFICATION`.
- Treat undated pages, caches, news, forums, and third-party summaries as secondary unless no primary source exists.
- Check later documents and superseding policies.
- Never convert historical or proposed requirements into current requirements.

Do not output verified for unsupported claims.
