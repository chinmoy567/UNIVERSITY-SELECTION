## States and universities (as grouped in Notion)

- 1. Western Australia: Curtin University, Edith Cowan University, Murdoch University, University of Western Australia
- 2. South Australia: University of Adelaide, Flinders University
- 3. New South Wales: Charles Darwin University, Charles Sturt University, Macquarie University, Southern Cross University, University of New South Wales, University of Wollongong, UTS
- 4. Victoria: CQUniversity, Deakin University, Federation University Australia, Monash University, RMIT, Victoria University
- 5. Queensland: Griffith University, James Cook University, University of Queensland, University of Southern Queensland, University of the Sunshine Coast
- 6. Australian Capital Territory: Australian National University
- 7. Tasmania: University of Tasmania

## Notes on the Notion grouping (kept as Notion shows it)

- Charles Darwin University is in the Northern Territory, but Notion lists it under New South Wales.
- CQUniversity is a Queensland university, but Notion lists it under Victoria.
- "University of Queensland" appears twice in Notion: once under New South Wales (blank page) and once under Queensland (blank page). Only the Queensland entry has a folder.
- Notion heading "Canbbera" is treated as Australian Capital Territory.
