## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Applied Science in Software Engineering (MAScSE), St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador. [Program page, updated 2026-10-27](https://mun.ca/become/graduate/programs-and-courses/software-engineering/) (OFFICIAL)

## 2. Duration and structure

- 4 terms, 16 months, full-time; Fall intake only. [Program page](https://mun.ca/become/graduate/programs-and-courses/software-engineering/) (OFFICIAL, 2026-10-27). Part-time: Unverified.
- Co-op / internship: Not found / not published.
- Total 30 credit hours; courses are 3 credits each (10 courses). [Calendar 8.7](https://www.mun.ca/previous-university-calendars/2026-2027/school-of-graduate-studies/school-of-graduate-studies/8/7/) (OFFICIAL; the program page itself does not list credits)
- Breakdown: core 18 ch (6 courses) + capstone ENGI 9837 (3 ch) + electives 9 ch. The engineering page says "core 21 ch incl. capstone + electives 9 ch", which is the same total, not a real conflict.
- Minimum 65% in each course; failing a core course means repeating it; below 65% in two courses means withdrawal (Calendar 8.7, OFFICIAL).

## 3. Admission requirements

- Limited, competitive entry on overall academic performance. Normally a second-class four-year bachelor's in computer science, computer engineering or a related field from a university of recognized standing. [Calendar 8.7](https://www.mun.ca/previous-university-calendars/2026-2027/school-of-graduate-studies/school-of-graduate-studies/8/7/) (OFFICIAL). The [program page](https://mun.ca/become/graduate/programs-and-courses/software-engineering/) confirms "computer science, computer engineering, or a cognate area" (OFFICIAL).
- 4-year bachelor's accepted: Yes (it is required).
- Numeric minimum GPA / percentage: Not found / not published (only "second class"). [Apply page, updated 2026-11-19](https://www.mun.ca/become/graduate/apply/) (OFFICIAL)
- Prerequisite courses, work experience, portfolio, average admitted GPA: Not found / not published. Supervisor: not needed (course-based). GRE: not listed for this program.
- References: 2 letters (academic or professional; not family or friends). Statement of Interest: part of the online application, weighed heavily by most units. CV required. [Apply page](https://www.mun.ca/become/graduate/apply/) (OFFICIAL)
- Documents: transcripts, confirmation of degree or anticipated completion letter (so provisional is accepted), CV, proof of English; non-English transcripts need an official or notarized translation ([Apply page](https://www.mun.ca/become/graduate/apply/), OFFICIAL).

Do I meet it? Unclear. CGPA 3.34 (about 66.64%, not First Class) probably lands in the "second class" range, but MUN does not publish a cutoff or how it converts the BAIUST scale. Ask admissions, and check that the degree is in a related field.

## Full Official Program Information

Thesis check: course-based, four terms, no thesis. Thesis route: none exists. Exchange rate used: 1 CAD = 88.69 BDT (Wise mid-market, undated cached page; other sites show about 86-90; checked 2026-11-27). All fees below are international rates.

### Program overview (Faculty of Engineering page)

- Course-based Master of Applied Science in Software Engineering (MASc.SE), offered jointly by the Faculty of Engineering and Applied Science and the Faculty of Science.
- Four terms, no thesis component; a capstone course is taken instead.
- For students with a computer science or computer engineering background who want to specialize in software engineering.
- Graduates are prepared for software development, computer systems architecture and design, and distributed services roles.
- Intake: once a year, in the Fall.
- Taking more courses than required incurs additional tuition under university policy.

Source: https://www.mun.ca/engineering/graduate/programs/software-engineering/ (checked 2026-11-27)

### Admission (Graduate Calendar 8.7.1)

- Admission is limited, competitive, and based on overall academic performance.
- Applicants normally need a minimum second-class four-year bachelor's degree in computer science, computer engineering, or a related field from a recognized university.
- Applicants must meet the School's English Proficiency Requirements.
- The Dean of Graduate Studies grants admission on the recommendation of the program chair or either department head, along with a tentative program of study.

Source: https://www.mun.ca/previous-university-calendars/2026-2027/school-of-graduate-studies/school-of-graduate-studies/8/7/ (checked 2026-11-27)

| Core | 18 | COMP 6901, COMP 6905, ENGI 9818, ENGI 9867, ENGI 9874, ENGI 9839 |
| Electives | 9 | Three courses from the elective list below |

The engineering page lists the same courses as "seven core courses (21 credits)" including the capstone. Same total.

### Evaluation (Calendar 8.7.3)

- A grade of at least 65% is required in every program course for credit toward the program.
- A failed core course must be repeated. A failed elective must be repeated or replaced with another program course.
- Less than 65% in two courses, or in a repeated course, requires withdrawal from the program.
