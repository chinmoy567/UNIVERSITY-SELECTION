## Full details (original notes)

Last checked: 2026-11-22. Labels: OFFICIAL = university or government site; THIRD-PARTY = other. Page dates are the "last updated" dates shown on each page.

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Applied Science (MASc) in Computer Engineering, Faculty of Engineering and Applied Science, Memorial University of Newfoundland (MUN). [Program page](https://www.mun.ca/engineering/graduate/programs/computer-engineering/masc/) (OFFICIAL, updated 2026-05-07)
- Route: course-based with a project, no thesis ([program page](https://www.mun.ca/engineering/graduate/programs/computer-engineering/masc/); [Calendar 8.2](https://www.mun.ca/university-calendar/school-of-graduate-studies/school-of-graduate-studies/8/2/), OFFICIAL).
- Campus and city: St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador ([Engineering graduate admissions](https://www.mun.ca/become/graduate/programs/engineering.php), OFFICIAL, updated 2026-10-27).

## 2. Duration and structure

- About 2 years; intake Fall only ([program page](https://www.mun.ca/engineering/graduate/programs/computer-engineering/masc/), OFFICIAL, 2026-05-07). Full-time or part-time: Not found / not published.
- Co-op / internship: Not found (course-based project only).
- Total 33 credit hours: 5 core x 3 + 5 electives x 3 + project 3 (ENGI 981A 0 cr, ENGI 981B 3 cr, last two semesters). 10 courses plus project; each course 3 credits ([program page](https://www.mun.ca/engineering/graduate/programs/computer-engineering/masc/), OFFICIAL).
- ENGI 9818 and 9819 must each be passed with at least 65% before registering in other courses. Extra courses cost extra tuition (same page).

## 3. Admission requirements

- Background: a second-class degree in computer engineering, information and computer science, electrical and information engineering, computer information technology or a relevant area, from a recognized university ([program page](https://www.mun.ca/engineering/graduate/programs/computer-engineering/masc/), OFFICIAL, 2026-05-07). Engineering page: bachelor's with at least second class or "B" standing ([page](https://www.mun.ca/become/graduate/programs/engineering.php), OFFICIAL, 2026-10-27). Admission is limited and competitive ([Calendar 8.2](https://www.mun.ca/university-calendar/school-of-graduate-studies/school-of-graduate-studies/8/2/), OFFICIAL).
- Numeric minimum GPA/percentage and the Bangladesh equivalent of "second class": Not found / not published. Average admitted GPA: Not found.
- 4-year bachelor's: the page only says "a bachelor's degree"; Unverified whether a 4-year degree is explicitly required.
- Documents ([SGS how to apply](https://www.mun.ca/become/graduate/apply/), OFFICIAL, updated 2026-11-19): online application, transcripts and degree confirmation, two academic reference letters, CV, English proof if required, self-declared average. GRE: not listed for this program (Unverified). SOP: not listed. Supervisor: none (course-based). Work experience, original vs provisional transcripts: Not found.

Do I meet it? Unclear. CGPA 3.34 (about 66.64%, below 70%) may or may not count as "second class / B" for a BAIUST degree; MUN publishes no numeric cut-off or Bangladesh conversion. Ask the university before paying the fee.
