## 1. Basics

- Program: MSc Computer Science, course route (Option 2, all-course). [Program page](https://www.mun.ca/computerscience/graduate-students/graduate-programs/msc-course-route/) (OFFICIAL, updated Feb 06, 2026)
- Degree: Master of Science, course-based (non-thesis). Department of Computer Science, Faculty of Science. [Program overview](https://www.mun.ca/become/graduate/programs-and-courses/computer-science/) (OFFICIAL, updated Oct 27, 2026)
- Campus/city: St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador. Application code MS/COMP/COUR ([info for applicants](https://www.mun.ca/computerscience/graduate-students/info-for-applicants/), OFFICIAL, Feb 13, 2026)

## 2. Duration and structure

- Length: 4 semesters (16 months) full-time; calendar says 2 years full-time, 4 years part-time ([Calendar 32.9](https://www.mun.ca/university-calendar/school-of-graduate-studies/school-of-graduate-studies/32/9/), OFFICIAL, 2026-27). Intakes: Fall (Sept) and Winter (Jan); no Spring intake for MSc ([info for applicants](https://www.mun.ca/computerscience/graduate-students/info-for-applicants/), OFFICIAL).
- Credits: minimum 30 credit hours, at least 21 in Computer Science, other 9 CS or approved electives (Calendar 32.9, OFFICIAL). At least 10 courses, 7 COMP ([program page](https://www.mun.ca/computerscience/graduate-students/graduate-programs/msc-course-route/), OFFICIAL). 3 credit hours per course is inferred (10 x 3 = 30).
- Co-op: optional full-time PAID work term of 1 or 2 semesters, competitive, usually adds one semester; pay amount Not found ([program page](https://www.mun.ca/computerscience/graduate-students/graduate-programs/msc-course-route/), OFFICIAL).
- No thesis, project or seminar in the course route. 65% rule: at least 65% in every program course; two grades below 65% means withdrawal (Calendar 32.9, OFFICIAL).

## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum: bachelor's with major in Computer Science or Computer Engineering and 75% overall average and/or Upper Second Class; Head of Department may waive when circumstances warrant ([Calendar 32.9](https://www.mun.ca/university-calendar/school-of-graduate-studies/school-of-graduate-studies/32/9/), OFFICIAL; same on [program overview](https://www.mun.ca/become/graduate/programs-and-courses/computer-science/), OFFICIAL, Oct 27, 2026).
- Conflict – verify with the university: course-route page says CS or related background (Computer Engineering, IT, other computing science) while the calendar says major in CS or Computer Engineering.
- Canadian equivalent: percentage (75%). BAIUST 3.34/4.0 conversion Not published; dept refers to the WES guide ([FAQ](https://mun.ca/computerscience/graduate-students/application-process-frequently-asked-questions), OFFICIAL, updated 2022, old).
- 4-year bachelor's: bachelor's required; "4-year" wording Not found.
- GRE: calendar says international applicants "strongly encouraged"; FAQ says highly recommended, not required (OFFICIAL).
- References: 2 letters of appraisal; referees not family, friends or students ([apply page](https://www.mun.ca/become/graduate/apply-to-memorial/), OFFICIAL, Nov 19, 2026). Transcripts uploaded after applying; original vs provisional Not found.
- SOP, CV, portfolio, work experience, prerequisite courses, supervisor (none for course route), average admitted GPA: Not found / not published.
- Funding: none for course-route students ([program page](https://www.mun.ca/computerscience/graduate-students/graduate-programs/msc-course-route/), OFFICIAL).

**Do I meet it? No** on the published 75% minimum (CGPA 3.34 is about 66.64%). Unclear only if the Head of Department waives it or a WES-style conversion lifts the grade; also the degree must be in CS/Computer Engineering. Unclear on field match.

### Program page (course route)
Source: https://www.mun.ca/computerscience/graduate-students/graduate-programs/msc-course-route/ (checked 2026-11-27)

- 4 semesters (16 months), start in September or January.
- Expected: Bachelor's in Computer Science or a related field, such as Computer Engineering or Information Technology.
- At least 10 courses, at least 7 in Computer Science. The other 3 may be electives in CS or related disciplines approved by the Graduate Studies Committee.

| Term | Start in Fall | Start in Winter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sep-Dec | 2 COMP courses | 3 COMP courses |
| Jan-Apr | 3 COMP courses | 2 COMP courses |
| May-Aug | 2 elective courses | 2 elective courses |
| Sep-Dec | 2 COMP and 1 elective | 3 COMP courses |
| Jan-Apr | 2 COMP and 1 elective | 2 COMP and 1 elective |

- Co-op: after three semesters, some students may enrol in work term courses. Full-time, paid, 1 or 2 semesters (4 or 8 months), one employer. Limited, competitive, selective. A work term normally extends the program by one semester.
- Funding: no financial aid for course-based students; they must support themselves.

### Graduate calendar 32.9 (Computer Science)
Source: https://www.mun.ca/university-calendar/school-of-graduate-studies/school-of-graduate-studies/32/9/ (checked 2026-11-27)

- Admission (32.9.1): Bachelor's with major in Computer Science or Computer Engineering, minimum 75% overall average and/or Upper Second Class standing. Head of Department may waive in special circumstances. International applicants encouraged to submit GRE.
- Option 2 (course-based): at least 30 credit hours, at least 21 in Computer Science. The remaining 9 may be approved related CS courses or other courses approved by the Graduate Studies Committee.
- Full-time students expected to finish in 2 years, part-time in 4. Each work term adds one semester.
- Minimum 65% in each program course. Two courses below 65% (or one failed repeated course) means withdrawal.
- Route change allowed once, after 12 credit hours.
- Co-op work terms COMP 611W and 612W: competitive; 12 credit hours completed before and 3 remaining after.
