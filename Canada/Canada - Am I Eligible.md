## 4. How I ranked

I ranked in this order:
1. Does a published rule **fail** me? (math grade, 70% minimum, degree type, closed intake) → bottom.
2. Is the overall minimum **3.0 / B average**, and does my **last 2 years** (about 3.47) clear it? → top.
3. Is the program free of math-grade rules? (my math is weak) → higher.
4. Is the rule only **"second class" with no number**, or not stated? → middle. Needs an email.
5. Cost and IELTS break ties.

| Tier | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **A** | Best chances on paper. Email first. |
| **B** | Possible. Unclear rule. Email first. |
| **C** | Leaning fail. Apply only if the university confirms in writing. |
| **D** | Fails a published rule. Do not apply. |
