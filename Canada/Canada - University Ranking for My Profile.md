## The 3 rules I used to rank

| # | Rule | My result | What I did |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Overall percentage rule** (example: "70% overall") | 66%, below 70 | Programs that publish 70% or more go to the bottom |
| 2 | **Last-2-years rule** (example: "75% in last 2 years", or "3.0 in last 60 credits") | 68%, below 70 and 75 | Percent rules fail. GPA rules (3.0) are borderline |
| 3 | **Math rule** (B or B+ in math) | 1 of 6 math courses is B+ or better | Programs with a math rule go below programs with none |

Then I looked at IELTS (7.0+ is a risk), thesis (skip), and cost.

**Order of ranking:**
1. No published percentage rule and no math rule → top
2. GPA rule of 3.0 (about "B"). Borderline, because 68% may convert to just under B → middle
3. Published percentage above mine (70%, 73–76%, 75%) → bottom
4. Percentage fail plus a math fail, or admission suspended → very bottom

> **Honest note:** "No published rule" does not mean "safe". It means the university does not say. Email them first. No program is a sure "Yes".
