## 1. Basics

- Program: MSc Applied Computer Science and Society (MSc-ACS), University of Winnipeg, Winnipeg, Manitoba. [Program page](https://www.uwinnipeg.ca/programs/msc-acs.html) (OFFICIAL, not readable, 403) and [Graduate Calendar PDF, updated 2026-04-23](https://www.uwinnipeg.ca/academics/graduate-calendar/docs/grad-msc-acs.pdf) (OFFICIAL)

- Department: Applied Computer Science. Graduate Program Chair: S. Ramanna (Graduate Calendar PDF, OFFICIAL).

## 2. Duration and structure

- Expected 2 years; maximum 5 years. Intakes: September and January (Graduate Calendar PDF, OFFICIAL).
- Full-time or part-time: Unverified. Fee pages list a full-time international fee.
- Co-op / internship: Not found / not published.
- Thesis route: minimum 12 credit hours (4 courses, GACS-7xxx/3) plus GACS-7500 Graduate Thesis (credit value not stated) (OFFICIAL).
- Course-based route: minimum 21 credit hours of GACS-7xxx/3 (7 courses) plus minimum 9 credit hours of GACS-4xxx/3 (3 courses) = about 30 credit hours, 10 courses (arithmetic from the stated minimums) (OFFICIAL).
- Each course is 3 credit hours. No second-language requirement. Route switch allowed with supervisor approval ([Calendar PDF](https://www.uwinnipeg.ca/academics/graduate-calendar/docs/grad-msc-acs.pdf)).
