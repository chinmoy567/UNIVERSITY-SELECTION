## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Artificial Intelligence and Business Analytics (MAIBA), Cape Breton University. [Program page](https://www.cbu.ca/academics/programs/master-of-artificial-intelligence-and-business-analytics/) (OFFICIAL)
- Degree type and route: master's degree, in person, course-based with a final-semester choice of a 420-hour internship or a Master's project ([program page](https://www.cbu.ca/academics/programs/master-of-artificial-intelligence-and-business-analytics/), OFFICIAL). Label "professional master's" is my reading, Unverified.
- Department: Shannon School of Business ([program page](https://www.cbu.ca/academics/programs/master-of-artificial-intelligence-and-business-analytics/), [CBU news, 16 Mar 2026](https://www.cbu.ca/newsroom/releases/cape-breton-university-launches-three-new-masters-programs/), OFFICIAL).
- Campus and city: CBU campus, Sydney, Nova Scotia (program page says on campus; city not shown in the text I read, Unverified).

## 2. Duration and structure

- Length: 16 months full-time, 36 credits; part-time available (contact [ssobgradstudies@cbu.ca](mailto:ssobgradstudies@cbu.ca)) ([program page](https://www.cbu.ca/academics/programs/master-of-artificial-intelligence-and-business-analytics/), OFFICIAL).
- Terms: 4 semesters, Fall, Winter, Spring/Summer, Fall ([sample planner PDF](https://www.cbu.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/MAIBA-Program-Sample-Schedule-2026.pdf), OFFICIAL). Fall to Fall fits 16 months. Fall 2026 intake advertised ([news](https://www.cbu.ca/newsroom/releases/cape-breton-university-launches-three-new-masters-programs/)); Fall 2027 deadlines posted. Winter or Spring intakes: Not found.
- Conflict resolved: THIRD-PARTY [Kanan](https://www.kanan.co/study-in-canada/universities/cape-breton-university-cbu/masters-program-master-of-artificial-intelligence-and-business-analytics/) says 2 years; the official page and the official planner (4 semesters) both say 16 months. Use 16 months.
- Credits: 27 credits core (9 courses), 6 credits experiential learning (Master's Project MGSC-6600 or Internship MGSC-6625), 3 credits elective = 36 ([planner PDF](https://www.cbu.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/MAIBA-Program-Sample-Schedule-2026.pdf), OFFICIAL). Core courses are 3 credits each (27/9, arithmetic).
- Co-op: no co-op as such. Internship option is 420 hours; whether it is paid: Not found.

## 3. Admission requirements

- Background: four-year undergraduate degree (or equivalent) in science, mathematics, computer science, business, economics or engineering. A 4-year bachelor's is accepted. ([Program page](https://www.cbu.ca/academics/programs/master-of-artificial-intelligence-and-business-analytics/), OFFICIAL)
- Minimum GPA: 3.0 (73-76%) or B in the final 60 credits (same page, OFFICIAL).
- Prerequisite: a statistics course with at least 70% final grade (same page, OFFICIAL).
- Exception: applicants who do not meet the criteria may be considered on GMAT and/or professional experience (CV) (same page, OFFICIAL).
- GRE, references, SOP, portfolio, supervisor, transcript type, average admitted GPA: Not found / not published. Work experience is not required on the page.

Do I meet it? No on the stated minimum (CGPA 3.34 on the BAIUST scale is about 66.64%, below 73-76%); possible only through the GMAT/professional-experience exception. Statistics course at 70%+ and degree field: Unclear.
