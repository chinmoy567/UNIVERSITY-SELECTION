## 1. Basics

- Program: MSc Computer Science, Jodrey School of Computer Science, Acadia University. [CS graduate page](https://cs.acadiau.ca/graduate.html) (OFFICIAL, read 2026-11-22, no update date shown)
- Degree: MSc, three routes: thesis, project, course-based. Routes of interest: project-based and course-based ([source](https://cs.acadiau.ca/graduate.html), OFFICIAL).
- Campus and city: Wolfville, Nova Scotia; delivered in person ([source](https://cs.acadiau.ca/graduate.html), OFFICIAL).
- Department: Jodrey School of Computer Science ([Guide Before You Apply](https://cs.acadiau.ca/grad_guide.html), OFFICIAL).

## 2. Duration and structure

- Course-based: normally 16 months. Project-based: normally 2 years ([source](https://cs.acadiau.ca/graduate.html), OFFICIAL). Third-party lists 24 months ([Mastersportal](https://www.mastersportal.com/studies/146831/computer-science.html), THIRD-PARTY).
- Intakes: Fall (Sept), Winter (Jan), Summer (May-Aug) at university level (search summary of official admissions page, Unverified). CS-specific intake list: Not found / not published.
- Full-time or part-time: Unverified (pages describe full-time terms).
- Co-op: MSc students who completed two terms and have supervisor approval can do a 4 to 8 month paid industry co-op ([CS Graduate FAQ](https://cs.acadiau.ca/graduate-faq.html), OFFICIAL).
- Project route: 15 credit hours advanced coursework + COMP 5923 (Research Methods) + COMP 5950 (Project) ([source](https://cs.acadiau.ca/graduate.html), OFFICIAL). Total not stated.
- Course route: 21 credit hours advanced coursework + COMP 5923 (same source). Courses are 3 credit hours (inferred: 15 ch = 5 courses), so 7 electives + COMP 5923 = 8 courses, about 24 ch (inference).
- Rules: at least one course each from applications, systems, theory; up to one-third may be cross-listed with undergraduate courses (same source).

## 3. Admission requirements

- Degree: bachelor's in Computer Science or equivalent (e.g. Computer Engineering) ([Guide Before You Apply](https://cs.acadiau.ca/grad_guide.html), OFFICIAL). General rule: honours or four-year bachelor's (search summary, Unverified). A 4-year bachelor's is accepted if it is CS or equivalent.
- Prerequisites: all nine courses (COMP 2113, 3343, 3403, 3613, 3713, 3753; MATH 1013, 1253, 1413) with B- (70%) or better. Up to two missing may be considered if a faculty member wants to supervise; the FAQ says up to 3 make-up courses above 70% ([Guide](https://cs.acadiau.ca/grad_guide.html), [FAQ](https://cs.acadiau.ca/graduate-faq.html), OFFICIAL).
- Minimum GPA: B- (2.67) average in all required CS and Math courses ([FAQ](https://cs.acadiau.ca/graduate-faq.html), OFFICIAL). University minimum 2.67/4.0, or 70% in major-field courses over the last two undergraduate years ([Graduate application](https://www2.acadiau.ca/admissions/graduate-application.html), OFFICIAL). Canadian equivalent used for Bangladesh: Not found.
- Supervisor: required before applying for the thesis route only ([Guide](https://cs.acadiau.ca/grad_guide.html), OFFICIAL). Faculty interest helps if prerequisites are missing. Possible technical online interview.
- Documents: online application, one official transcript per institution (unofficial or emailed not accepted), resume, letter of intent, two references via the online system ([Graduate application](https://www2.acadiau.ca/admissions/graduate-application.html), OFFICIAL, general). MSc CS applicants also complete a Departmental Information form (search summary, Unverified). Original vs provisional certificate: Not found.
- GRE: "may be required" generally, CS not stated: Not found. Work experience, portfolio, average admitted GPA: Not found / not published.
- Process: rolling review; may close when capacity is reached; funding is limited ([Guide](https://cs.acadiau.ca/grad_guide.html), OFFICIAL).
