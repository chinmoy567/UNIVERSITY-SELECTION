## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Applied Computer Science (MACSc; "MACS" on Faculty pages). [Program page](https://www.dal.ca/academics/programs/graduate/applied-computer-science.html) OFFICIAL
- Degree type: professional, course-based master's (not MSc/MEng, no thesis). Options: Project, Internship, Entrepreneurship. [Courses page](https://www.dal.ca/study/program-sites/applied-computer-science/program-overview/courses.html) OFFICIAL
- Department: Faculty of Computer Science. Campus: Halifax, Nova Scotia, full-time, in person. [Program page](https://www.dal.ca/academics/programs/graduate/applied-computer-science.html) OFFICIAL
- Note: the program page "why choose" text mentions the Master of Digital Innovation (looks like a copy error). Unverified, confirm with [graduate@cs.dal.ca](mailto:graduate@cs.dal.ca).

## 2. Duration and structure

- Length: "16 months or longer without scheduled breaks"; the full-time program fee covers 12 months. [Program page](https://www.dal.ca/academics/programs/graduate/applied-computer-science.html) OFFICIAL
- Full-time only. Part-time: Not found / not published.
- Intakes: September, January, May (3 terms per year). May intake is not offered to applicants who must take CSCI 5990 (Fall only). [Admissions](https://www.dal.ca/study/program-sites/applied-computer-science/admissions.html) OFFICIAL
- Co-op / internship: Internship stream (CSCI 9100, 3 cr) and Entrepreneurship stream (CSCI 9200). Paid or unpaid: Not found / not published. [Courses page](https://www.dal.ca/study/program-sites/applied-computer-science/program-overview/courses.html) OFFICIAL
- Total: 30 credit hours, 3 credits per course, about 10 courses. Core 9 cr (3 courses). Internship stream: 9 core + 18 elective + 3 work term. Entrepreneurship: 9 + 6 (two BUSI courses) + 12 elective + 3. Project: 9 + 15 elective + 6 (Research Project 1 and 2). [Courses page](https://www.dal.ca/study/program-sites/applied-computer-science/program-overview/courses.html) OFFICIAL
- Fee model: program fee (flat per term, not per credit). [Fee schedule](https://www.dal.ca/content/dam/www/admissions/cost-and-payment/tuition-and-fee-schedules/masters-tuition-fee-schedule.pdf) OFFICIAL

## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum GPA: 3.0 (Dal 4.3 scale) in technical courses; meeting the minimum does not guarantee admission. Screening looks at the last two years. [Admissions](https://www.dal.ca/study/program-sites/applied-computer-science/admissions.html) OFFICIAL
- Percentage equivalent: Not found / not published. The page points international applicants to "Select International GPA Equivalencies" (Bangladesh entry Unverified, check it) and says only top-30 Chinese universities use Dal's conversion.
- Background: four-year bachelor's or equivalent (accepted) with second-year core CS knowledge; non-CS applicants may need qualifying courses; CSCI 5990 may be added. Away from academia: recent industry experience needed. [Admission process](https://www.dal.ca/study/program-sites/applied-computer-science/admissions/macs-admission-process-and-application-state.html) OFFICIAL
- Documents: 2 reference letters (institution or company email, or letterhead), statement of intent, CV, unofficial transcripts for every institution (official copies after offer: Unverified). GRE, portfolio, average admitted GPA: Not found / not published. [Admissions](https://www.dal.ca/study/program-sites/applied-computer-science/admissions.html) OFFICIAL
- Mandatory online interview (about 30 min; reply within 2 business days). Supervisor: not required. [Interview](https://www.dal.ca/study/program-sites/applied-computer-science/admissions/macs-admission-interview.html) OFFICIAL
- Do I meet it? Unclear. CGPA 3.34 (about 66.64%) is likely near or above a 3.0 cut-off only if Dal converts BAIUST 3.34/4.0 to about 3.0 or higher on its 4.3 scale; conversion is not published, the CS-technical GPA and degree field are unknown, and the interview decides.
