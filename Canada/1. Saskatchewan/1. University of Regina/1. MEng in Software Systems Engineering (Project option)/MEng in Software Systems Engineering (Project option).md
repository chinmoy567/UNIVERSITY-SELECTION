## Full details (original notes)

Last checked: 2026-11-22. Labels: OFFICIAL = university or government site; THIRD-PARTY = other. No [uregina.ca](http://uregina.ca) page shows a "last updated" date (fee schedule is dated Oct 16, 2026). Items not confirmed on a page are marked Unverified.

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Engineering (MEng), Software Systems Engineering, Project option. University of Regina, Regina, Saskatchewan (on campus). [Program page](https://uregina.ca/academics/programs/engineering/masters-phd-software-systems-engineering.html) (OFFICIAL, undated)
- Degree type: professional master's (MEng). Other routes: MEng Co-op, MASc (thesis), PhD. [Graduate calendar](https://uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/graduate-calendar/all-programs/engg-sse.html) (OFFICIAL)
- Route: project (ENSE 902, over two semesters). Faculty of Engineering and Applied Science. The program page describes it as for practising engineers seeking career growth. (OFFICIAL)

## 2. Duration and structure

- Length: 2 years, 30 credit hours (Co-op version: 38 credits, 2 years 8 months). [Program page](https://uregina.ca/academics/programs/engineering/masters-phd-software-systems-engineering.html) (OFFICIAL)
- Credits: 30 = seven 3-credit courses (21) + ENGG 701/702/703 at 1 credit each (3) + ENSE 902 project, 3 credits x 2 semesters (6). Only two courses may be undergraduate level (300-400). [Calendar](https://uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/graduate-calendar/all-programs/engg-sse.html) (OFFICIAL, re-checked 2026-11-22)
- Intakes: Fall and Winter (see Deadlines). Number of terms: not published. About 5 terms implied by 30 credits at 6 per term (Unverified inference).
- Full-time: 6 credits (2 courses) per term. [Tuition and fees](https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/future-students/tuition-and-fees.html) (OFFICIAL). International students are expected to study full-time in Fall/Winter (engineering graduate newsletter via search summary, Unverified).
- Co-op: only in the separate Co-op route (co-op fee 1,149 CAD per term). The Project route has no co-op. [Fee schedule](https://www.uregina.ca/financial-services/assets/202710-grad-studies-tuition-schedule-oct-16.pdf) (OFFICIAL)

## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum: 70% graduating average from a four-year bachelor's (75% for English, History, Math/Stat, MPA). Lower averages may be considered if the last two complete years are 75% or more. Programs are competitive. [Eligibility](https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/future-students/eligibility-requirements.html) (OFFICIAL, re-checked). Engineering-specific minimum: not published on the program calendar page. Canadian equivalent used: 70% = 2.67/4.00 (WES iGPA, Stage 1, Unverified).
- Four-year bachelor's expected; 3-year degrees only with qualifying courses. Applicants lacking background may be admitted as qualifying students (extra courses in year one). [Calendar](https://uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/graduate-calendar/all-programs/engg-sse.html) (OFFICIAL). Specific prerequisite courses: Not found / not published.
- References: 2 official letters (academics or employers; not family, friends or current grad students; institutional email). Letter of Intent (SOP) and CV/resume uploaded. [How to apply](https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/future-students/how-to-apply.html) (OFFICIAL, re-checked)
- Transcripts: unofficial copies for review, official required later. WES ICAP course-by-course evaluation required for Engineering applicants with international education. Original vs provisional certificate: Not found / not published. (OFFICIAL, same page)
- Supervisor: not required for MEng (contacting professors is recommended). [Engineering graduate page](https://www.uregina.ca/engineering/graduate-students/Index.html) (OFFICIAL). GRE, work experience, portfolio, average admitted GPA: Not found / not published.

Do I meet it? No on the face of the rule (unclear). CGPA 3.33 is about 66%, below the 70% minimum and below First Class. The only route is the exception (last two years at 75% or more), and that is not confirmed for a BAIUST transcript. Confirm the percentage conversion with the university.

## Full Official Program Information

Thesis check (2026-11-27): the MEng (project) is course-based. The calendar says: "The MEng is effectively a course-based program of which the project is a required course." No thesis and no research supervisor needed (a project supervisor is assigned). The MASc (thesis) and PhD routes need a supervisor and are skipped here. ### Program description (official)
Source: https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/graduate-calendar/all-programs/engg-sse.html (checked 2026-11-27)

Associate Dean, Graduate Studies and Research: Raman Paranjape, PhD. Graduate Program Coordinator: Craig Gelowitz, PhD. For more information about Graduate programs, contact engg.grad@uregina.ca.

"Software Systems Engineering's objective is to train students to study, analyze, design and develop different software systems including web-based software systems, transaction-based systems, interactive multimedia systems, and management information systems, and to become knowledgeable in the process and life-cycle aspects of software development. The program also aims to teach students to apply computational theories to real-life programming techniques; analyze requirements; design, implement and test software systems; plan and manage software projects; solve technical problems; practice co-design and embedded systems construction."

### Admissions (official calendar wording)
Source: https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/graduate-calendar/all-programs/engg-sse.html (checked 2026-11-27)

Qualifying students are expected to become fully qualified students within their first year of studies."

### How to apply, deadlines, documents
Source: https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/future-students/how-to-apply.html (checked 2026-11-27)

- Portal opens the year before the start: Winter first week of February, Spring/Summer first week of April, Fall first week of August.
- Engineering and Applied Science deadlines: **January 31 (Fall); July 31 (Winter)**. Late applications are not accepted. The year is not stated.
- Review: FGSR about 1 to 2 weeks, faculty up to 45 days, Dean 3 to 7 days.
- Application fee: CAD 125 (≈ BDT 11,288), non-refundable, paid online by MasterCard, VISA or American Express (no prepaid or debit cards). One application and fee per program.
- Documents: online application; unofficial transcripts for review, official transcripts from all institutions later; WES ICAP course-by-course evaluation (strongly recommended for all international credentials); resume; Letter of Intent; letters of recommendation; official test scores; program Supplementary Materials. Do not send unrequested documents.
- References: at least two official letters from academics or employers; not family, friends, educational consultants or current graduate students; give an institutional email for each referee.
