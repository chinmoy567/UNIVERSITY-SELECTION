## 1. Basics

- Program: MSc in Computer Science, course-based route, Data Science (DS) or Human-Centred Computing (HCC). University of Regina, Regina, Saskatchewan. [Calendar](https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/graduate-calendar/all-programs/computer-science.html) (OFFICIAL) · [DS program page](https://uregina.ca/science/computer-science/graduate-programs/msc-cs-data-science.html) (OFFICIAL) · [Admission page](https://www.uregina.ca/science/computer-science/graduate-programs/application-process.html) (OFFICIAL)
- Degree type: MSc, course-based with capstone project (no thesis). Department: Computer Science, Faculty of Science; applications via Faculty of Graduate Studies and Research (FGSR). Full-time ([program overview](https://www.uregina.ca/academics/programs/science/master-phd-computer-science.html), OFFICIAL).
- Status: Calendar lists DS (course) and HCC (course) as "temporarily suspended"; the older generic MSc course route "suspended effective 202130" ([Calendar](https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/graduate-calendar/all-programs/computer-science.html), OFFICIAL). Conflict – verify with the university ([CS@uregina.ca](mailto:CS@uregina.ca), [grad.science@uregina.ca](mailto:grad.science@uregina.ca)).

## 2. Duration and structure

- Length: 2 years (program overview, OFFICIAL). DS plan = 5 semesters: Fall, Winter, Spring/Summer, Fall, Winter ([DS page](https://uregina.ca/science/computer-science/graduate-programs/msc-cs-data-science.html), OFFICIAL). HCC term plan: Not found.
- Intake: DS = Fall; HCC = Winter (admission page, OFFICIAL). One cohort a year each.
- Part-time: Not found / not published.
- Co-op: optional designation. Needs full-time registration, at least two CS 800-level courses, minimum 80% average, competitive selection, core courses done before any work term; co-op fee CAD 1,115.50 ([tuition schedule](https://www.uregina.ca/financial-services/assets/202610-grad-studies-tuition-schedule-nov-6-25.pdf), OFFICIAL). Whether work terms are paid: Not found.
- Total 30 credits. DS: CS 700 and CS 710-716 (8 courses x 3 = 24), CS 718 seminar (0), CS 719 project (6). HCC: CS 700, CS 730-736 (24), CS 738 (0), CS 739 (6). 3 credits per course (Calendar, OFFICIAL).

## 3. Admission requirements

- Background: four-year bachelor's in Computer Science; other disciplines need an equivalent BSc CS plus 10 CS courses at 300/400 level (1 theory, 2 software, 2 systems, 2 applications) ([admission page](https://www.uregina.ca/science/computer-science/graduate-programs/application-process.html), OFFICIAL). Four-year bachelor's accepted.
- Minimum: overall average 70% and CS average 75%; meeting the minimum often is not enough because of demand. Applications with grades below B-level are automatically rejected (same page, OFFICIAL).
- FGSR general rule: 70% from a four-year bachelor's; a lower average may be considered if the last two complete years are 75%+. 70% = 2.67/4.0 on the WES iGPA calculator ([eligibility](https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/future-students/eligibility-requirements.html), OFFICIAL).
- International: WES transcript verification required (admission page). GRE: not required. Work experience and portfolio: Not found.
- References: FGSR needs at least 2 letters from academics or employers (not family, friends, current grad students), institutional email ([how to apply](https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/future-students/how-to-apply.html), OFFICIAL). Letter of intent (SOP) and resume required.
- Supervisor: not needed in advance for DS and HCC (admission page).
- Transcripts: unofficial copies for review; official transcripts or degree certificate needed for a conditional offer (how to apply). Provisional vs original certificate: Not found.
- Average admitted GPA: Not found / not published.
- Do I meet it? No / Unclear. CGPA 3.34 (about 66.64%) is below the 70% overall and 75% CS minimums on a simple conversion. The final answer depends on the WES conversion of BAIUST grades, the CS average, and whether the program is open at all. Treat as unlikely unless WES converts higher or the last-two-years 75% exception applies.
