## 2. Duration and structure

- Length: 2 years. [Overview](https://www.uregina.ca/academics/programs/science/master-phd-computer-science.html) (OFFICIAL). Full-time or part-time: Unverified (co-op needs full-time).
- Intakes: Fall (Feb 15), Winter (Jun 15), Spring/Summer (Oct 15) per application page (see Section 6).
- Total 30 credits: 21 coursework (min 7 courses x 3 cr) + 9 project research (CS 902) + two non-credit CS 900 seminars. [Program page](https://www.uregina.ca/science/computer-science/graduate-programs/msc-cs-project.html) and [calendar](https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/graduate-calendar/all-programs/computer-science.html) (OFFICIAL)
- Course mix: four CS 800-level, one CS course, two CS or pre-approved non-CS; max two 700-level (OFFICIAL).
- Credits per course: 3; full-time = 2 courses (6 cr). [Tuition page](https://www.uregina.ca/graduate-studies-research/future-students/tuition-and-fees.html) (OFFICIAL)
- Co-op (optional): full-time, 2 UofR CS 800-level courses, minimum 80%, supervisor permission, competitive; paid status Not found. (OFFICIAL, program page)

## 3. Admission requirements

- Degree: four-year bachelor's in Computer Science accepted; other degrees need equivalent BSc CS plus 10 CS 300/400-level courses. [Application process](https://www.uregina.ca/science/computer-science/graduate-programs/application-process.html) (OFFICIAL)
- Minimum: overall 70% and CS average 75%; meeting it is often not enough. International: 70% = 2.67/4.00 (WES); grades below B-level automatically rejected. (OFFICIAL, same page). The calendar page lists no admission minimum (only 80% for co-op).
- Supervisor: required for project route (OFFICIAL).
- GRE: not required. Recommendation letters: confidential, number not stated. Letter of intent, transcripts, WES report for international applicants (OFFICIAL).
- Work experience, CV, portfolio, original vs provisional certificates, average admitted GPA: Not found / not published.
- Do I meet it? No. CGPA 3.34 (about 66.64%) is below the 70% overall minimum; CS average unknown (75% needed) and the route may be suspended.
