- Program: Systems and Technology (MEST), W Booth School of Engineering Practice and Technology, Faculty of Engineering. [Program page](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca/sept/degree-options/systems-technology/) (OFFICIAL) and [eng.mcmaster.ca/?p=25701](https://eng.mcmaster.ca/?p=25701) (OFFICIAL)

- Full-time recommended 16 months (minimum 12, maximum 24). Part-time 40 months, domestic students only. [Program page](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca/sept/degree-options/systems-technology/) (OFFICIAL)
- Intakes: September and January (deadline tables, see section 6). Number of terms: Not found / not published.
- Co-op: optional, 4 to 12 months, needs chair approval, ENG 701 Graduate Co-op Prep in September. Whether co-op is paid: Not found / not published. [eng.mcmaster.ca/?p=25701](https://eng.mcmaster.ca/?p=25701) (OFFICIAL)
- Total 30 credit units, each course 3 units ("30 credit units are required to complete this degree"). (OFFICIAL)
- Project route: 8 courses (24 units) plus SEP 799 Part I and II (3 + 3 units). Courses-only route: 10 courses (30 units). Advanced standing up to 2 courses with Associate Dean approval. [eng.mcmaster.ca/?p=25701](https://eng.mcmaster.ca/?p=25701) (OFFICIAL)
