## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Engineering (MEng) in Computing and Software, McMaster University. [Program page](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca/cas/degree-options/computing-and-software-meng/) (OFFICIAL, no date) and [Graduate Studies listing](https://gs.mcmaster.ca/program/computing-and-software/) (OFFICIAL, no date).
- Degree: professional MEng, course plus independent project, no thesis ([program page](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca/cas/degree-options/computing-and-software-meng/), OFFICIAL).
- Department: Computing and Software, Faculty of Engineering.
- Campus and city: Hamilton, Ontario; John Hodgins Engineering Building, 1280 Main St W (program page, OFFICIAL).

## 2. Duration and structure

- Length: 2 years on the [program page](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca/cas/degree-options/computing-and-software-meng/); 1 to 2 years on the [GS listing](https://gs.mcmaster.ca/program/computing-and-software/) (both OFFICIAL, re-checked 2026-11-22). Regulations: courses within 16 months of entry, project about 4 months ([CAS regulations PDF, dated 2025-11-05](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca//app/uploads/2026/09/CAS_reg_meng_2025.pdf), OFFICIAL). Conflict – verify with the university: 2 years vs 1 to 2 years. The regulations fit the shorter end, so plan for 1.5 to 2 years.
- Full-time or part-time: both mentioned (GS listing and regulations, OFFICIAL).
- Intake: September (GS listing, OFFICIAL). The Engineering [how-to-apply page](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca/future-students/future-graduate-students/how-to-apply/) lists Jan/May/Sept deadlines for Faculty programs generally; whether this MEng takes January or May starts is Unverified.
- Structure: six one-term (half) graduate courses plus a project; max two at 600-level, max two from other departments; project examined by supervisor plus second examiner, pass/fail ([regulations PDF](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca//app/uploads/2026/09/CAS_reg_meng_2025.pdf), OFFICIAL).
- Co-op: 4 to 12 months co-op available to master's students ([program page](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca/cas/degree-options/computing-and-software-meng/), OFFICIAL). Paid or not: Not found / not published.
- Total credits and credits per course: Not found / not published (6 courses plus project). Unverified.
- Specializations: Computer Systems; Health Informatics and Bioinformatics; Scientific Computing and Optimization; Security, Privacy and Data Analytics; Software Quality; Theory and Methodologies of Computation (program page, OFFICIAL).

## 3. Admission requirements

- Background: Honours Bachelor's in Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics or equivalent ([CAS Graduate Information sheet, 2025-11-03](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca//app/uploads/2025/11/CAS-Graduate-Information-1.pdf), OFFICIAL). Other fields with substantial computing background are considered (regulations, OFFICIAL).
- Minimum grade: the program page says anticipated admission range "Min B- (70-72%)" ([program page](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca/cas/degree-options/computing-and-software-meng/), OFFICIAL, re-checked 2026-11-22; same in the info sheet). The regulations say an average of at least B on all 3rd/4th-level courses ([regulations PDF](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca//app/uploads/2026/09/CAS_reg_meng_2025.pdf), OFFICIAL, 2025-11-05). The GS listing states no minimum grade. Conflict – verify with the university: B- (70-72%) vs B. Neither value is below 70%.
- Low GPA: applicants may apply but need strong support from a prospective supervisor before submitting ([FAQ 2024 PDF](https://www.eng.mcmaster.ca//app/uploads/2024/03/Application_FAQ_Graduate_Computing-and-Software.pdf), OFFICIAL, 2024).
- Expected prerequisites: data structures, algorithms, programming languages, software design, logic/discrete math, automata, calculus, linear algebra, statistics (regulations, OFFICIAL).
- Is a Bangladeshi 4-year bachelor's accepted? Not found (Honours Bachelor's required; country equivalency page blocked). Unverified.
- Required: official transcripts and degree certificates (electronic first; originals or diploma copy later), statement of interest, CV, English scores if applicable, two academic references (professional allowed if graduated more than 5 years ago). GRE not considered. Applicants name 3 potential supervisors and admission needs a supervisor's acceptance (FAQ 2024 and info sheet, OFFICIAL). Work experience and portfolio: not required per lists. Average admitted GPA: Not found.
- Admission is competitive and rolling until full (FAQ 2024, OFFICIAL).

Do I meet it? No / Unclear. CGPA 3.34 (about 66.64%) is below the 70-72% anticipated minimum (and below B). It is not a hard rejection (low-GPA applicants may apply with strong supervisor support), but it is a reach, and the Bangladeshi 4-year degree equivalency is also unconfirmed.
