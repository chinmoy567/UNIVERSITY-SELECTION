## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Engineering (MEng), Systems Design Engineering, University of Waterloo, Faculty of Engineering. [Program page](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-graduate-students/programs/by-faculty/engineering/systems-design-engineering-master-engineering-meng) (OFFICIAL)
- Degree type: professional master's, coursework-based with an optional research project; no supervisor required. [Program page](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-graduate-students/programs/by-faculty/engineering/systems-design-engineering-master-engineering-meng), [FAQ](https://uwaterloo.ca/systems-design-engineering/graduate-studies/future-students/faq) (OFFICIAL)
- Campus and city: Waterloo, Ontario, on campus (Program page, OFFICIAL).

## 2. Duration and structure

- 16 months full-time; FAQ says four terms, possibly three. Part-time available (one course per term; full-time is at least two). Intakes: Fall, Winter, Spring (3 per year). ([Program page](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-graduate-students/programs/by-faculty/engineering/systems-design-engineering-master-engineering-meng), [FAQ](https://uwaterloo.ca/systems-design-engineering/graduate-studies/future-students/faq), OFFICIAL)
- Co-op/internship: not mentioned for this program. Unverified whether it is a co-op supported program (the \$836 co-op fee applies only to supported programs).
- Credits: 8 graduate courses (2 core + 6 electives, 0.50 unit each, about 4.0 units, derived). Core: SYDE 600 and one SYDE 660 workshop. At least 2 SYDE courses at 500/600/700 level, at most 2 at 500 level. Source: [archived Fall 2022 calendar](https://uwaterloo.ca/gsac-archive/archive-fall-2022/engineering/department-systems-design-engineering/master-engineering-meng-systems-design-engineering.html) (OFFICIAL, old); the FAQ confirms "at least 8 courses". Unverified for the current year (current calendar page could not be read).
- Funding: self-funded; MEng coursework students are not eligible for internal funding (Program page, FAQ, OFFICIAL).

## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum: 75% overall standing in a four-year honours bachelor's in engineering "or equivalent" (non-Canadian degrees); Canadian: 75% in the last two years. [Program page](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-graduate-students/programs/by-faculty/engineering/systems-design-engineering-master-engineering-meng) (OFFICIAL)
- Non-engineering degrees: must show engineering training via the supplementary information form and CV (Program page, OFFICIAL).
- Bangladesh: Waterloo's table lists "First Class standing in all degrees, 60/100" for a 4-year bachelor's. It is a guideline and departments may set higher requirements. [Equivalencies](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-graduate-students/admissions/information-international-students/international-admission-equivalencies) (OFFICIAL). No CGPA conversion is published there.
- Prerequisite courses, work experience, portfolio: not found / not required. GRE: not mentioned on the program page; the 2022 calendar required it for some applicants, current status Unverified.
- References: 2, at least 1 academic; 2 professional allowed if the degree was finished 5+ years ago. SOP: program-specific questions plus supplementary information form; CV required (Program page, OFFICIAL).
- Original vs provisional transcripts: not found. Applicants still finishing get a conditional offer ([FAQ](https://uwaterloo.ca/systems-design-engineering/graduate-studies/future-students/faq), OFFICIAL). Average admitted GPA: not published.
- Application fee: CAD 125 per program, non-refundable, cannot be waived (FAQ, OFFICIAL). Seat deposit: Not found / not published.

Do I meet it? No (likely). CGPA 3.34 is about 66.64%, below the program's 75% and not First Class (the Bangladesh guideline asks for First Class). It passes only if the department treats 3.34 as 75% or higher, which is not published, so confirm with the department.
