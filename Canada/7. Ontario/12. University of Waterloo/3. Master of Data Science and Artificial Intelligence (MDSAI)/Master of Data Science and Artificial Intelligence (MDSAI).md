## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (MDSAI), Co-op full-time option. [Program page](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-graduate-students/programs/by-faculty/math/data-science-and-artificial-intelligence-co-op-master-data) (OFFICIAL)
- Degree: professional master's, coursework route, no thesis, project optional ([program page](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-graduate-students/programs/by-faculty/math/data-science-and-artificial-intelligence-co-op-master-data), OFFICIAL). Non-co-op full-time or part-time option also exists ([program overview](https://uwaterloo.ca/data-science/masters-data-science-and-artificial-intelligence-mdsai), OFFICIAL).
- Department: Data Science, Faculty of Mathematics (OFFICIAL, program page).
- Campus: on-campus, Waterloo, Ontario.

## 2. Duration and structure

- Co-op: 4 terms / 16 months (study, study, 4-month work term May to August, study); optional 8-month work term gives 5 terms / 20 months ([overview](https://uwaterloo.ca/data-science/masters-data-science-and-artificial-intelligence-mdsai); [co-op requirements](https://uwaterloo.ca/data-science/current-graduate-students/mdsai-co-op-requirements), OFFICIAL; [FAQ](https://uwaterloo.ca/data-science/graduate-programs/frequently-asked-questions-faqs), OFFICIAL).
- Intake: Fall (September) only for co-op. Non-co-op: Fall, Winter, Spring considered ([deadlines](https://uwaterloo.ca/data-science/graduate-programs/application-deadlines), OFFICIAL).
- Co-op is paid, 12 to 16 weeks, not guaranteed; student finds the job; can switch to non-co-op if none ([FAQ](https://uwaterloo.ca/data-science/graduate-programs/frequently-asked-questions-faqs), OFFICIAL). Part-time students cannot work and are not PGWP-eligible (FAQ, OFFICIAL).
- Credits: Waterloo counts courses, not credits. 9 courses = 1 foundation + 5 core + 3 elective, plus Graduate AIM and an ethics requirement ([overview](https://uwaterloo.ca/data-science/masters-data-science-and-artificial-intelligence-mdsai), OFFICIAL; [archived 2023 calendar](https://uwaterloo.ca/gsac-archive/archive-fall-2023/mathematics/data-science-and-artificial-intelligence/master-data-science-and-artificial-intelligence-mdsai-co-operative-program-direct-entry.html), OFFICIAL archived). Credit-hours per course: Not found / not published.
- Standing: average of at least 75%, no course below 70% (archived 2023 calendar, Unverified for current year). WIL 601 required for co-op students ([co-op requirements](https://uwaterloo.ca/data-science/current-graduate-students/mdsai-co-op-requirements), OFFICIAL).

## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum average: 4-year honours bachelor's (or equivalent) with overall 78% (B+). "Competitive, actual cutoff much higher" ([admission requirements](https://uwaterloo.ca/data-science/graduate-programs/admission-requirements); [FAQ](https://uwaterloo.ca/data-science/graduate-programs/frequently-asked-questions-faqs), OFFICIAL; re-checked 2026-11-22). The general Waterloo minimum of 75% is lower; the program page governs ([general requirements](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-graduate-students/admissions/admission-and-english-language-proficiency-requirements), OFFICIAL).
- Bangladesh/CGPA conversion to a % scale: Not found (Waterloo equivalencies page returned 404). Unverified.
- Background: degree in data science, CS, statistics, math or related, with senior-level CS or statistics. Prerequisites: CS 115/116, STAT 230/231, MATH 136, MATH 137/138 (CS/Stats backgrounds); other backgrounds declare a subset of CO 250, CS 341, CS 348, STAT 330 to 333 equivalents. Gaps may mean admission on probation ([admission requirements](https://uwaterloo.ca/data-science/graduate-programs/admission-requirements), OFFICIAL; page cites 2019-20 calendar links, so verify).
- 4-year bachelor's: expected. Whether a Bangladeshi degree counts is decided via the equivalencies page (Unverified).
- Work experience: not required. GRE: not required (optional). References: 3, at least 2 academic. SOP: statement of interest up to 500 words, resume, program-specific questions ([program page](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-graduate-students/programs/by-faculty/math/data-science-and-artificial-intelligence-co-op-master-data), OFFICIAL).
- Transcripts: upload copies of all post-secondary transcripts; certified translation if not English; official documents may be requested on offer ([FAQ](https://uwaterloo.ca/data-science/graduate-programs/frequently-asked-questions-faqs), OFFICIAL). Original vs provisional certificate rule: Not found.
- Supervisor: not applicable (coursework). Portfolio: Not found. Average admitted GPA: Not published.
