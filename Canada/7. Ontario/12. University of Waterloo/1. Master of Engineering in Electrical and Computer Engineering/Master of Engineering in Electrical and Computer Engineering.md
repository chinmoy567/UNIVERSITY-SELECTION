## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum: 75% overall standing in a relevant 4-year Honours bachelor's or equivalent. International applicants: 75% across the degree; domestic: last two years ([dept page](https://uwaterloo.ca/electrical-computer-engineering/master-engineering-meng), [grad page](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-graduate-students/programs/by-faculty/engineering/electrical-and-computer-engineering-master-engineering-meng), [co-op page](https://uwaterloo.ca/electrical-computer-engineering/master-engineering-meng-co-op), OFFICIAL). The FAQ words it as the final two years ([FAQ](https://uwaterloo.ca/electrical-computer-engineering/graduate-studies/future-students/faq)); program pages say overall for international applicants: minor wording difference, verify.
- Bangladesh country page: "First Class standing in all degrees, 60/100", bachelor (4 year) or bachelor plus master; no CGPA conversion ([equivalencies](https://uwaterloo.ca/future-graduate-students/admissions/information-international-students/international-admission-equivalencies), OFFICIAL).
- Conflict (75% vs First Class/60): the same equivalencies page says departments or faculties "may have higher requirements for their programs." So the 75% program minimum is treated as the governing number and 60/100 as only the country floor. Still: Conflict – verify with the university (what 75% means for a BAIUST 3.34 CGPA; no conversion table published).
- 4-year bachelor's accepted: yes, in a relevant field (engineering, science, math; others case by case, FAQ OFFICIAL). 3-year degrees: not addressed. Prerequisite courses: Not published. Work experience: not required. GRE: not required, optional.
- Documents: transcripts, resume/CV, Supplementary Information Form (statement of interest), 2 references (at least one academic), English score unless exempt ([co-op page](https://uwaterloo.ca/electrical-computer-engineering/master-engineering-meng-co-op), OFFICIAL). Official transcripts only after an offer ([FAQ](https://uwaterloo.ca/electrical-computer-engineering/graduate-studies/future-students/faq)). Original vs provisional certificates, portfolio, average admitted GPA: Not found.

Do I meet it? No (likely). CGPA 3.34 is about 66.64%, below the 75% program minimum and not First Class. Unclear only because BAIUST-to-Waterloo conversion is unpublished; ask the university.
