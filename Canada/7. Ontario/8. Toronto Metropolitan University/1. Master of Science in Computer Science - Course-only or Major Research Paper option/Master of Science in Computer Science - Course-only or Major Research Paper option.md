## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: MSc Computer Science, Course-only or Major Research Paper (MRP) option (thesis option also exists). Toronto Metropolitan University (TMU), Faculty of Science, Department of Computer Science, downtown Toronto. [Program page](https://www.torontomu.ca/graduate/programs/computer-science/) (OFFICIAL), [Calendar](https://www.torontomu.ca/graduate/calendar/programs-and-courses/computer-science/) (OFFICIAL; labelled 2025-26, "last revised Aug 18, 2026").
- Format: full-time. The program page does not describe routes; the calendar does.

## 2. Duration and structure

- Course-only: 8 elective credits, no milestone. MRP: MRP plus master's seminar plus 6 elective credits (written proposal, supervisor and second reader). Thesis: thesis, seminar, CP8101 or CP8337, 3 electives. Most courses are 1 credit. Seminar: attend at least 10 seminars and give 1 oral presentation. AI specialization: 6 of 8 (course-only) or 4 of 6 (MRP) electives in AI. [Calendar](https://www.torontomu.ca/graduate/calendar/programs-and-courses/computer-science/) (OFFICIAL).
- Length: about 2 years for course-only (search snippet of a department page). Unverified. Program page does not state length or credits.
- Intakes: Fall only listed for 2027 ([application dates](https://www.torontomu.ca/graduate/future-students/application-dates/), OFFICIAL). Winter/Spring not listed.
- Co-op / internship: Not found / not published. Funding: program page says domestic thesis students are funded; none noted for course-only.

## 3. Admission requirements

- Degree: 4-year undergraduate degree in computer science or equivalent. Any-field 4-year graduates may qualify via the Coding, Programming and Algorithms certificate series or the Computer Coding certificate. Competitive. [Program page](https://www.torontomu.ca/graduate/programs/computer-science/) (OFFICIAL).
- Minimum GPA: 3.00/4.33 (B) in the last two years of study (same page, OFFICIAL).
- Bangladesh: B/3.00 equals 3.0/4 or 63%; bachelor's equivalent is a 4-year bachelor's; formal assessment at application. [Requirements](https://www.torontomu.ca/graduate/future-students/requirements/) (OFFICIAL, from Stage 1).
- Documents: statement of intent, CV, transcripts, 2 reference letters, English proof (program page, OFFICIAL). Official transcripts on sealed security paper from the Registrar, grading scale, notarized English translation plus originals ([FAQ](https://www.torontomu.ca/graduate/future-students/apply/faq/), OFFICIAL, Stage 1).
- GRE: not required for MSc CS (FAQ, OFFICIAL, Stage 1).
- Not found / not published: prerequisite courses, work experience, portfolio, supervisor requirement for course-only, provisional certificates accepted, average admitted GPA.

Do I meet it? Unclear. CGPA 3.34 (about 66.64%) is above 63% and above 3.0/4 on paper, but the BAIUST scale conversion, last-two-years GPA, a CS-related degree and competitiveness are unconfirmed, and international applications are currently closed for Fall 2027.
