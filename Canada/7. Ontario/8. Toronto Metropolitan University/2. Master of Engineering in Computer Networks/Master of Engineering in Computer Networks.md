## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Engineering (MEng) in Computer Networks, Toronto Metropolitan University (TMU), Toronto, ON. [Program page](https://torontomu.ca/graduate/programs/computer-networks) (OFFICIAL, no date)
- Route: course-based (coursework plus Master's Project/Case Study); MASc is the thesis route. [Calendar](https://torontomu.ca/graduate/calendar/programs-and-courses/computer-networks) (OFFICIAL, revised 2026-08-06)
- Department: Electrical, Computer and Biomedical Engineering, Faculty of Engineering and Architectural Science. [Dept page](https://www.torontomu.ca/computer-networks) (OFFICIAL)
- Campus: Toronto (87 Gerrard St E office). Same source.

## 2. Duration and structure

- Duration: FT 1 year minimum (max 3 years); PT 2 years (max 5). [Program page](https://www.torontomu.ca/computer-networks/program/) (OFFICIAL, no date). The main grad page does not state length.
- Intake: Fall 2027 FT open to international; PT international: no intake. Winter and Spring 2027 not listed for this program. [Application dates](https://www.torontomu.ca/content/ryerson/graduate/future-students/application-dates/) (OFFICIAL, as of 2026-11-20)
- Co-op/internship: Not found / not published.
- Credits: 12 = project CN8001 (2, Pass/Fail) + 6 core (six 1-credit courses) + 4 elective credits. Each course is 1 credit. [Calendar](https://torontomu.ca/graduate/calendar/programs-and-courses/computer-networks) (OFFICIAL, re-checked 2026-11-22)
- Conflict – verify with the university: program page says "nine course credits plus a major project worth one credit" ([program page](https://www.torontomu.ca/computer-networks/program/), OFFICIAL, undated) vs calendar 12 credits with a 2-credit project. Calendar is dated, so preferred.

## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum GPA: MEng 3.00/4.33 (B) in last two years (MASc 3.33/4.33, B+). Competitive; minimum does not guarantee offer. [Program page](https://torontomu.ca/graduate/programs/computer-networks) (OFFICIAL, re-checked)
- Background: 4-year bachelor's in engineering, computer science, mathematics or physical sciences (or equivalent). 4-year bachelor's accepted: yes. Same source.
- Documents: statement of interest, CV, transcripts, 2 recommendation letters, English proof. Same source.
- Supervisor: not needed for MEng. Prerequisites, GRE, work experience, portfolio, original vs provisional transcripts, average admitted GPA: Not found / not published.
- TMU conversion of Bangladesh percentage/BAIUST CGPA to the 4.33 scale: Not found. Unverified. THIRD-PARTY (collegedunia) claims 65% minimum; not reliable.

Do I meet it? Unclear (leaning Yes on the GPA minimum). 3.34 on a 4.0 scale is likely about B+ and above B (3.00/4.33), but TMU's own conversion for BAIUST is not published; ask the university. Field match also must be checked.
