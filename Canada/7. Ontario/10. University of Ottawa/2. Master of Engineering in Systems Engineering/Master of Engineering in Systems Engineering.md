## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Engineering in Systems Engineering (MEng), program without thesis (course-based, with project options), University of Ottawa, Faculty of Engineering, Ottawa, Ontario. Applicants must select "program without thesis" on the application. [Catalogue 2026-2027](https://catalogue.uottawa.ca/en/graduate/master-engineering-systems-engineering/) (OFFICIAL, snippet, Unverified). Former name: Master of Systems Science and Engineering.
- Program overview page: [uottawa.ca](https://www.uottawa.ca/faculty-engineering/graduate-studies/programs/systems-engineering) (OFFICIAL, blocked 402).
- Thesis alternative (separate program): [MASc Systems Engineering](https://www.catalogue.uottawa.ca/en/graduate/master-applied-science-systems-engineering/) (OFFICIAL).
- Department and exact campus building: Not found / not published.

## 2. Duration and structure

- Full-time or part-time: both ([Catalogue](https://catalogue.uottawa.ca/en/graduate/master-engineering-systems-engineering/), OFFICIAL, snippet, Unverified).
- Compulsory (3 units each): GNG 5301 Professional Skills and Responsibility, SYS 5100 Systems Engineering, SYS 5160 Systems Integration; then 6 units from a group including Industry Project, SYS 5180, SYS 5185, SYS 5186 (topics). Unverified.
- Conflict – verify with the university: [preview catalogue](https://uottawa-ca-preview.courseleaf.com/en/graduate/master-engineering-systems-engineering/) (OFFICIAL, preview) shows 15 optional units plus a Project in Systems Engineering, versus 9 compulsory + 6 chosen units above. The catalogue notes requirements changed from 2025-2026.
- Total credits, number of terms, intakes, co-op/internship (paid or not): Not found / not published. A uOttawa course is normally 3 units (from course codes, Unverified).

## 3. Admission requirements

- Bachelor's in Computer Science, Economics, Engineering, Mathematics, Operations Research, Science or a related area, minimum average B (70%). Missing courses are normally an admission condition. Minimums do not guarantee admission. [Catalogue](https://catalogue.uottawa.ca/en/graduate/master-engineering-systems-engineering/) (OFFICIAL, snippet, Unverified).
- General uOttawa graduate rule: honours (four-year) bachelor's or equivalent, 70% (B) average on the last 20 courses; some programs set higher ([How to apply](https://www.uottawa.ca/study/graduate-studies/how-to-apply), OFFICIAL, snippet, Unverified). So a 4-year bachelor's is accepted.
- Bangladesh conversion of 3.34 / 66.64%: Not found. Confirm with the university.
- GRE, references, SOP, CV, supervisor, transcript format (original/provisional), average admitted GPA, work experience: Not found / not published.
- Ignore THIRD-PARTY Collegedunia claims (1.7/4.0, GRE/GMAT, 3.0 GPA): they concern other programs and conflict with the official 70%.

Do I meet it? No (likely). CGPA 3.34 (about 66.64%) is below the stated 70% (B) minimum; only a university-specific Bangladesh conversion could change this. Treat as Unclear until the university confirms the conversion.
