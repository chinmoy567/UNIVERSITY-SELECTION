## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Applied Computing (MAC), coursework (professional, course-based) option. [Program page](https://www.wlu.ca/programs/science/graduate/applied-computing-mac/) (OFFICIAL, no page date)
- Route: coursework, 9 courses including a graduate project. Thesis (Waterloo) and paid co-op (Waterloo) are separate options, not this one. (Program page, OFFICIAL)
- Department: Physics and Computer Science, Faculty of Science with the Faculty of Graduate and Postdoctoral Studies. [Program requirements](https://students.wlu.ca/programs/science/computer-science-and-physics/graduate-students/program-requirements/index.html) (OFFICIAL)
- Campus: full-time coursework in Brantford, Ontario; part-time coursework in Brantford or Online. (Program page, OFFICIAL)

## 2. Duration and structure

- Full-time: 4 terms (16 months). Part-time: 5 to 9 terms (20 to 36 months). Intakes: September, January, May (3 terms a year). (Program page, OFFICIAL)
- Co-op / internship: none in the coursework option. The paid 8-month co-op exists only in the separate Waterloo co-op option, chosen at application (Program page, OFFICIAL).
- Structure (full-time): CP600 Practical Algorithm Design (in person) + CP683 Graduate Project + seven 600-level electives = 9 courses. (Program page, OFFICIAL)
- Total credits and credits per course: Not found / not published. Both pages give course counts only and point to the academic calendar. Unverified.

## 3. Admission requirements

- Background: four-year honours degree in Computer Science or a closely related area, clearly covering data structures, object-oriented programming, programming and software engineering. (Program page, OFFICIAL)
- Minimum: final-year GPA at least B+ AND cumulative B+ in all computer science courses. Applicants below this can take the Post-Baccalaureate Certificate in Computer Science and be considered with a B+ average there. (Program page, OFFICIAL, re-checked)
- General graduate rule: second-class honours / B standing or equivalent, four-year honours bachelor's or equivalent. [Apply page](https://www.wlu.ca/Academics/faculties/graduate-and-postdoctoral-studies/admissions/apply/index.html) (OFFICIAL, from Stage 1)
- Canadian equivalent of BAIUST 3.34 (about 66.64%): Not found / not published. B+ is usually about 77-79% in Ontario (Unverified, general knowledge, no Laurier Bangladesh conversion found).
- 4-year bachelor's accepted: Yes ("four-year honours or equivalent"). Bangladesh-specific assessment: Not found.
- Coursework extras: transcripts for all undergraduate and graduate work. References, SOP, CV, portfolio and supervisor are listed only for thesis/co-op, not for coursework. GRE, work experience, average admitted GPA: Not found / not published. (Program page, OFFICIAL)
- Transcripts (as stated for thesis/co-op, probably similar): dated within 6 months, one PDF per institution, certified translation if not in English, degree or provisional certificate with grading scale, WES not accepted. (Program page, OFFICIAL)
- Assessment is rolling until the deadline or until the program is full; only complete applications are assessed. (Program page, OFFICIAL)

Do I meet it? Unclear. CGPA 3.34 (about 66.64%) is probably below the B+ cutoff, but the page allows discretionary review and the post-baccalaureate route. Ask [gradadmissions@wlu.ca](mailto:gradadmissions@wlu.ca) how they convert BAIUST grades.
