## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Data Science (MDS), University of Guelph, Guelph, Ontario. [Program page](https://www.uoguelph.ca/programs/master-of-data-science/) (OFFICIAL, no date) and [Graduate Calendar](https://calendar.uoguelph.ca/graduate-calendar/graduate-programs/data-science/) (OFFICIAL, 2026-27)
- Degree type: Master of Data Science (professional, coursework-based, in person). No thesis or MRP; optional project course DATA\*6700 (1.00 credit). (Program page, OFFICIAL)
- Department: Mathematics and Statistics recommends admission; MDS office in Summerlee Science Complex, Room 1313. (Program page, OFFICIAL)

## 2. Duration and structure

- Full-time 12 months (another section of the page says 12 to 16 months); part-time 24 months, up to 6 semesters. [Program page](https://www.uoguelph.ca/programs/master-of-data-science/) OFFICIAL
- Intake: Fall (September) only, no Winter entry. (Program page, OFFICIAL)
- Co-op / internship: none found; project DATA\*6700 has no guaranteed funding. (Program page, OFFICIAL)
- Total credits: minimum 4.00 graduate credits. [Calendar](https://calendar.uoguelph.ca/graduate-calendar/graduate-programs/data-science/) OFFICIAL
- Breakdown: 4 core courses (2.00) + 2 MDS restricted electives (1.00) + 2 capstone courses (1.00) = 8 courses of 0.50 credit; DATA\*6900 is 0.00. Geospatial field: 4 core + 2 Geospatial electives + DATA\*6700 (1.00).
- Credit-unit note: 0.50 credit = one semester course. The program page lists DATA\*6100 to 6600 as six required 0.50 courses, slightly different wording from the calendar; the calendar is more detailed.

## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum: honours bachelor's (or equivalent) with 75% (B) average in the last four semesters. Below 75% is considered only with extenuating circumstances in the Statement of Academic Intent. ([Program page](https://www.uoguelph.ca/programs/master-of-data-science/), [Calendar](https://calendar.uoguelph.ca/graduate-calendar/graduate-programs/data-science/), both OFFICIAL)
- Conflict – verify with the university: older FAQ (Fall 2022 data) says about 70% with circumstances ([FAQ](https://www.uoguelph.ca/programs/master-of-data-science/faq/), OFFICIAL but older). Current pages say 75%; prefer 75%.
- Canadian equivalent for Bangladesh: Not found / not published. 4-year bachelor's: "honours bachelor's or equivalent" required; Bangladesh equivalence Unverified.
- Background: major or minor in data science, CS, math, stats or related, or equivalents of STAT\*3240 and CIS\*2500. Unrelated honours degree: Diploma in Applied Statistics with 75% first. (Calendar, OFFICIAL)
- GRE/GMAT and WES: not required. Work experience and supervisor: not required ([FAQ](https://www.uoguelph.ca/programs/master-of-data-science/faq/), OFFICIAL). Statement of Academic Intent: required.
- Reference letters, CV, portfolio, original vs provisional transcripts, average admitted GPA: Not found / not published (Unverified).
