## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Engineering in Software Engineering (Co-op option), Faculty of Engineering and Applied Science, Ontario Tech University, North Oshawa campus, Oshawa, Ontario, in-class. [Program page](https://ontariotechu.ca/programs/graduate/engineering-and-applied-science/masters-software-engineering-co-op/index.php) (OFFICIAL)
- Degree: MEng, course-based (10 courses). A project-based variant (7 courses + ENGR 5002G) is only available by transfer after at least 1 term and needs a faculty supervisor. [Grad Studies page](https://gradstudies.ontariotechu.ca/future_students/programs/masters_programs/software_engineering/index.php) (OFFICIAL)
- Department: Faculty of Engineering and Applied Science (FEAS). Same sources.

## 2. Duration and structure

- 16 months full-time; accelerated format possible; part-time possible. [Program page](https://ontariotechu.ca/programs/graduate/engineering-and-applied-science/masters-software-engineering-co-op/index.php), [Grad Studies page](https://gradstudies.ontariotechu.ca/future_students/programs/masters_programs/software_engineering/index.php) (OFFICIAL)
- Intakes: September and January (May is for MASc only). Same sources.
- Co-op: optional, chosen on the application; one 4-month term or two consecutive (max 8 months); full-time and paid. [Program page](https://ontariotechu.ca/programs/graduate/engineering-and-applied-science/masters-software-engineering-co-op/index.php) (OFFICIAL)
- Courses: 10 (3 core + 7 electives). Credits: 30 (3 per course) per the [tuition page](https://registrar.ontariotechu.ca/fees-and-payment/international-student-tuition/graduate-international-tuition-2026-2027.php) (OFFICIAL); the program page does not state credits.
- Core: ENGR 5013G Advanced Engineering Mathematics; ENGR 5201G Engineering Communication and Ethics; ENGR 5410G Project Management for Engineers.
- Electives: Conflict – verify with the university. Program page: one each from Software Engineering, Software Systems and General, plus 4 from any category. Grad Studies page: 3 Software Engineering + 1 Software Systems + 3 from any category. Links above.

## 3. Admission requirements

- Background: four-year honours bachelor's in software engineering, computer science or a relevant engineering field from an accredited Canadian program or equivalent recognised institution. Four-year bachelor's accepted. [Program page](https://ontariotechu.ca/programs/graduate/engineering-and-applied-science/masters-software-engineering-co-op/index.php) (OFFICIAL)
- Minimum: B overall (GPA 3.0 on a 4.3 scale) and B in the last two full-time years (4 semesters). [Grad Studies page](https://gradstudies.ontariotechu.ca/future_students/programs/masters_programs/software_engineering/index.php) (OFFICIAL)
- Bangladesh percentage equivalent for "B": Not found / not published. The [international credentials page](https://gradstudies.ontariotechu.ca/future_students/international_applicants/international-credentials/index.php) says credentials are assessed individually ([connect@ontariotechu.ca](mailto:connect@ontariotechu.ca)). THIRD-PARTY context only (University of Toronto, not this school): Bangladesh B = 60%.
- GRE, work experience, prerequisite courses, portfolio: Not found / not published. Supervisor: not needed for course-based MEng.
- Documents: letters of recommendation, transcripts, statement of academic intent, degree certificates (number of referees, original vs provisional: Not found). [International applicants](https://gradstudies.ontariotechu.ca/future_students/international_applicants/index.php) (OFFICIAL)
- Average admitted GPA: Not found. Admission is competitive; meeting the minimum does not guarantee a place.
- Do I meet it? Unclear. CGPA 3.34 on the 4.0 BAIUST scale (about 66.64%) is above 3.0 numerically, but Ontario Tech's B is on a 4.3 scale and its Bangladesh conversion is not published. Also confirm the "B in last two years" rule and that the degree field is relevant.
