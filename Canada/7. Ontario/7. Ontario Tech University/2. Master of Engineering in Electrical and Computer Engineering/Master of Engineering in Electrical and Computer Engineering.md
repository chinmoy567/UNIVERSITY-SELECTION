## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Engineering (MEng) in Electrical and Computer Engineering, professional master's. [Program page](https://gradstudies.ontariotechu.ca/future_students/programs/masters_programs/electrical_and_computer_engineering/index.php) (OFFICIAL)

- Faculty of Engineering and Applied Science, North Oshawa campus, 2000 Simcoe Street North, Oshawa, Ontario. In-class, full-time or part-time. (same page, OFFICIAL)
- Department: Electrical, Computer and Software Engineering. Unverified (seen only in site navigation).

## 3. Admission requirements

- Degree: four-year honours engineering degree in a relevant field from an accredited Canadian program, or an equivalent from a recognized institution. A 4-year BSc Engineering from Bangladesh is the same type. ([Program page](https://gradstudies.ontariotechu.ca/future_students/programs/masters_programs/electrical_and_computer_engineering/index.php), OFFICIAL)
- Minimum: overall B ("GPA 3.0 on a 4.3 scale or 73 to 76 per cent") and B in the last two full-time years (four semesters). Competitive; the minimum does not guarantee admission. (same page, OFFICIAL, re-checked)
- Bangladesh-specific conversion: Not found / not published. Average admitted GPA: Not found / not published.
- References: two letters of recommendation (referee type not stated). Statement of academic intent: 1-2 pages. Transcripts: unofficial transcript and degree photocopy at application, official transcripts only after an offer. ([Documents](https://gradstudies.ontariotechu.ca/future_students/application_process_and_requirement/step%203/index.php), OFFICIAL, Stage 1 reading; [FAQ](https://gradstudies.ontariotechu.ca/future_students/application_process_and_requirement/faqs.php) confirms official transcripts on acceptance)
- GRE, work experience, CV, portfolio, prerequisite courses: Not found / not published. Supervisor: not needed for course-based, needed for the project option.
- Conflict – verify with the university: how a Bangladeshi 4.0-scale CGPA is converted. 3.34 on the BAIUST scale is about 66.64%, below the 73-76% floor. The program page says 3.0 "on a 4.3 scale"; a 3.34/4.0 CGPA would be above 3.0 if read as a GPA. Do not assume; ask the university.

Do I meet it? Unclear, leaning No. By percentage (66.64% vs 73-76%) it does not meet the minimum. By GPA it may (3.34 vs 3.0), depending on how the university converts the BAIUST scale.
