## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Applied Computing (MAC), with an Artificial Intelligence stream variant. [Graduate Studies page](https://www.uwindsor.ca/graduate-studies/305/applied-computing) (OFFICIAL), [Program page](https://www.future.uwindsor.ca/program/master-of-applied-computing/) (OFFICIAL), [AI stream page](https://www.future.uwindsor.ca/program/master-of-applied-computing-mac-artificial-intelligence-stream/) (OFFICIAL)
- Degree type and route: professional, course-based master's (no thesis). Coursework plus an approved work-placement internship/project (COMP-8967, COMP-8977), or a supervised on-campus project if no placement. [AI stream page](https://www.future.uwindsor.ca/program/master-of-applied-computing-mac-artificial-intelligence-stream/) (OFFICIAL)
- Department: School of Computer Science, Faculty of Science; admitted through Faculty of Graduate Studies. [Graduate Studies page](https://www.uwindsor.ca/graduate-studies/305/applied-computing) (OFFICIAL). Exact department wording: Unverified.
- Campus and city: Windsor, Ontario (401 Sunset Avenue, N9B 3P4). [Program page](https://www.future.uwindsor.ca/program/master-of-applied-computing/) (OFFICIAL)

## 2. Duration and structure

- Total credits: 30 credit hours = 10 courses at 3.0 each. [AI stream page](https://www.future.uwindsor.ca/program/master-of-applied-computing-mac-artificial-intelligence-stream/) (OFFICIAL). Unverified for the general (non-AI) MAC, because the general pages do not state credits.
- Length: suggested sequence is 4 terms (about 16 months); the internship can be extended by one 4-month term. [AI stream page](https://www.future.uwindsor.ca/program/master-of-applied-computing-mac-artificial-intelligence-stream/) (OFFICIAL). The 2026/27 fee schedule also charges the program per semester over 4 semesters ([APC fee package, Apr 9 2026](https://www.uwindsor.ca/secretariat/sites/uwindsor.ca.secretariat/files/apc260409p_-_combined_april_9_2026_apc_package.pdf), OFFICIAL, rates labelled proposed).
- Intakes: 3 per year, Summer, Fall, Winter. [Graduate Studies page](https://www.uwindsor.ca/graduate-studies/305/applied-computing) (OFFICIAL)
- Full-time or part-time: Not found / not published.
- Co-op / internship: approved work-placement internship (COMP-8977, 3 credits); a 12-week "Transform" employment-readiness program is mentioned. Whether the internship is paid: Not found / not published. [Program page](https://www.future.uwindsor.ca/program/master-of-applied-computing/) (OFFICIAL)
- Credit breakdown: 8 core courses (including 2 internship/project courses) + 2 electives. [AI stream page](https://www.future.uwindsor.ca/program/master-of-applied-computing-mac-artificial-intelligence-stream/) (OFFICIAL)
- School credit-unit note: Not found. After 4 full-time semesters, a per-course fee applies (program fee divided by number of required courses) ([APC fee package](https://www.uwindsor.ca/secretariat/sites/uwindsor.ca.secretariat/files/apc260409p_-_combined_april_9_2026_apc_package.pdf), OFFICIAL).

## 3. Admission requirements

- Required degree: 4-year bachelor's in Computer Science or a closely related discipline. A 4-year bachelor's is accepted. Non-CS graduates may first complete UWindsor's one-year BCS for University Graduates with 70%+. [AI stream page](https://www.future.uwindsor.ca/program/master-of-applied-computing-mac-artificial-intelligence-stream/) (OFFICIAL), [Graduate Studies page](https://www.uwindsor.ca/graduate-studies/305/applied-computing) (OFFICIAL)
- Minimum grade: 70% major average, OR 77% or better over the last 3 years of study. The minimum does not guarantee admission. [Graduate Studies page](https://www.uwindsor.ca/graduate-studies/305/applied-computing) (OFFICIAL), re-checked 2026-11-22; same on [Program page](https://www.future.uwindsor.ca/program/master-of-applied-computing/) (OFFICIAL). General graduate minimum 70%: [Admission requirements](https://www.uwindsor.ca/graduate-studies/346/admission-requirements) (OFFICIAL).
- Conflict – verify with the university: "last 3 years" (Graduate Studies and Program pages) versus "last two years" (AI stream page). Links above.
- Competitiveness: 5 or more grades below 70% in CS courses significantly lowers chances; 2 or more backlogs in CS core courses means no admission. [Graduate Studies page](https://www.uwindsor.ca/graduate-studies/305/applied-computing) (OFFICIAL)
- Prerequisite courses, average admitted GPA, BAIUST/Bangladesh percentage conversion: Not found / not published.
- Work experience: not listed as a requirement. GRE: only if credentials are difficult to assess ([Admission requirements](https://www.uwindsor.ca/graduate-studies/346/admission-requirements), OFFICIAL).
- Documents: Statement of Interest, CV/resume, 2 letters of reference ([Graduate Studies page](https://www.uwindsor.ca/graduate-studies/305/applied-computing), OFFICIAL). The AI stream page says the statement is max 2 pages on a major technology project and an interview is part of admission (OFFICIAL).
- Transcripts: upload unofficial transcripts with grading scale at application; official paper transcripts only after an offer ([Admission requirements](https://www.uwindsor.ca/graduate-studies/346/admission-requirements), OFFICIAL). Original vs provisional certificate: Not found.
- Supervisor / portfolio: not required (course-based).

Do I meet it? No (likely). CGPA 3.34 (about 66.64%) is below the stated 70% major average, and the 77% last-3-years alternative is higher still. The BAIUST-to-percentage conversion is not published, so the exact figure is Unclear, but the risk is high.
