## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Applied Computing (MAC), Artificial Intelligence stream, University of Windsor. [Program page](https://www.future.uwindsor.ca/program/master-of-applied-computing-mac-artificial-intelligence-stream/) (OFFICIAL, no date)
- Degree: professional, course-based master's, no thesis. [Grad Studies page](https://www.uwindsor.ca/graduate-studies/305/applied-computing) (OFFICIAL)
- Route: 10 courses including COMP-8967 and COMP-8977 Internship/Project. AI stream = 2 AI courses + COMP-8977 on an approved AI topic. [Program page](https://www.future.uwindsor.ca/program/master-of-applied-computing-mac-artificial-intelligence-stream/) (OFFICIAL)
- Department: Faculty of Science, School of Computer Science (same page, OFFICIAL)
- Campus: 401 Sunset Avenue, Windsor, Ontario N9B 3P4 (OFFICIAL)

## 2. Duration and structure

- Length: 4 semesters (16 months). [MAC page](https://future.uwindsor.ca/master-applied-computing) (OFFICIAL, re-checked). The AI-stream page shows a four-term sequence but no length.
- Full-time / part-time: Not found / not published.
- Intakes: Summer, Fall, Winter ([MAC page](https://future.uwindsor.ca/master-applied-computing) OFFICIAL).
- Co-op / internship: COMP-8977 requires an approved internship; if none is found, a supervised on-campus project is assigned; extension up to 4 months costs extra ([program page](https://www.future.uwindsor.ca/program/master-of-applied-computing-mac-artificial-intelligence-stream/) OFFICIAL). Paid or not: Not found.
- Credits: 30 credit hours = 10 courses x 3.0 (OFFICIAL). 8 required (incl. COMP-8967, 8977) + 2 electives.
- Standing: 70% average needed to continue/graduate (OFFICIAL).
