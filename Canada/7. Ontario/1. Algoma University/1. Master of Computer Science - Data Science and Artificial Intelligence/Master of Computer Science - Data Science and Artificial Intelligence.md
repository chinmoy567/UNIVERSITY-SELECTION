## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Computer Science, Data Science and AI specialization (MCS.DSAI), Algoma University, Ontario. [Program page](https://algomau.ca/academics/graduate-programs/master-of-computer-science-16mth/) (OFFICIAL, no date). Launch news [posted 2026-05-01](https://algomau.ca/news/new-16-month-master-of-computer-science-specialization-at-algoma-university-bridges-talent-gap-with-hands-on-ethical-tech-training-in-ai-and-data-science) (OFFICIAL).
- Degree: professional, course-based Master of Computer Science (not MSc). Route: course-based with a two-term capstone (Computing Project I and II). [Program page](https://algomau.ca/academics/graduate-programs/master-of-computer-science-16mth/) (OFFICIAL).
- Department: Faculty of Computer Science and Technology (OFFICIAL, same page).
- Campuses: Sault Ste. Marie (1520 Queen St E) and Brampton (24 Queen St E). (OFFICIAL, same page.)
- Do not confuse with the separate [12-month MCS](https://algomau.ca/academics/graduate-programs/master-of-computer-science-12mth/) (no specialization) or the thesis MSc.

## 2. Duration and structure

- Length: 16 months, course-based. Intake: January 2027 (Winter 2027). [Program page](https://algomau.ca/academics/graduate-programs/master-of-computer-science-16mth/) (OFFICIAL).
- Full-time or part-time: part-time rates are listed on the [tuition page](https://algomau.ca/students/tuition-scholarships-financial-aid/graduate-programs-tuition-awards-and-funding/) (OFFICIAL) but whether the DSAI route allows part-time is Unverified. International students need full-time study for work rights and PGWP.
- Number of terms: not published. About 4 terms (Winter, Spring, Fall 2027, Winter 2028) is an inference. Unverified.
- Co-op / internship: not mentioned for this program. Unverified (the 12-month MCS page says no work placement).
- Credits: not published (the site counts courses). Structure: 6 MCS core + 3 specialization core (including Project I and II) + 3 electives = 12 courses. [Program page](https://algomau.ca/academics/graduate-programs/master-of-computer-science-16mth/) (OFFICIAL).
- Progression (12-month MCS page, may apply): minimum 70% per course and 70% cumulative average. [12-month page](https://algomau.ca/academics/graduate-programs/master-of-computer-science-12mth/) (OFFICIAL). Unverified for DSAI.

## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum grade: 70% (B) average over four years, OR 75% (B+) over the last two years of full-time study. The checklist on the page asks for the 75% last-two-years figure. [Program page](https://algomau.ca/academics/graduate-programs/master-of-computer-science-16mth/) (OFFICIAL, re-checked 2026-11-22).
- Canadian equivalent used for BAIUST CGPA: Not found / not published. WES evaluation is optional for non-North-American institutions.
- Background: four-year computer science or closely related degree from an accredited university. A 4-year bachelor's is required (accepted). (OFFICIAL, same page.)
- Prerequisites: Conflict – verify with the university. Admission text says any 3 of 15 foundational courses; the checklist lists six named courses (Introductory Programming, Introductory Statistics, Computer Organization, Data Structures II, Computer Networks, Theory of Computing). [Program page](https://algomau.ca/academics/graduate-programs/master-of-computer-science-16mth/) (OFFICIAL).
- Backlogs: two or more in CS core courses significantly reduce chances (OFFICIAL, same page).
- Work experience, GRE, reference letters, portfolio, supervisor: not required or not mentioned (course-based).
- Documents: application form, transcript (official transcripts requested later), language test. Optional: personal statement (up to 500 words), CV, WES. Original vs provisional certificates: Not found. (OFFICIAL, same page.)
- Average admitted GPA: Not found / not published. Minimums do not guarantee admission.

Do I meet it? Unclear, leaning No. CGPA 3.34 (about 66.64%) is below the 70% four-year average. The 75% last-two-years route needs strong final-two-year results, and Algoma's conversion of the BAIUST scale is unknown. Ask Algoma for a scale evaluation before applying.
