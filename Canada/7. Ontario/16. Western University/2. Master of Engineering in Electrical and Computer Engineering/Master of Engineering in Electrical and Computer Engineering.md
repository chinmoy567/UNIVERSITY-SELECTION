## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Engineering (MEng) in Electrical and Computer Engineering, Western University, London, Ontario. [Program page](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/electrical/graduate/current_students/meng_programs/index.html) (OFFICIAL, no date)
- Degree type and route: professional, course-based. Applicants can only apply to the course-based option; a project (MEng Project or Major Research Project) needs an ECE supervisor and approved proposal, and is not guaranteed. [How to apply](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/electrical/graduate/future_students/How-to-apply.html) (OFFICIAL)
- Streams listed: Electrical and Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Engineering in Medicine ([How to apply](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/electrical/graduate/future_students/How-to-apply.html), OFFICIAL). Conflict – verify with the university: the MEng page navigation still lists Communications, Power and Robotics streams; a search snippet said these merge into General ECE from Sept 2026 (Unverified).
- Department: Electrical and Computer Engineering, Faculty of Engineering. Thompson Engineering Building, Room 279, London ON N6A 5B9. [MEng page](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/electrical/graduate/current_students/meng_programs/index.html) (OFFICIAL)

## 2. Duration and structure

- Length: 3 terms (12 months), full-time. MEng-GDip is 4 terms (16 months). [How to apply](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/electrical/graduate/future_students/How-to-apply.html) (OFFICIAL). Part-time: Not found.
- Intakes: Conflict – verify with the university. ECE MEng page says students can start in September ([MEng page](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/electrical/graduate/current_students/meng_programs/index.html)); the Faculty page says three intakes a year from Sept 2026 (Winter Jan, Spring May, Fall Sept) ([Faculty rounds page](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/graduate/future-students/How-to-Apply-for-Admission/index.html)). Confirm with [ecegrad@uwo.ca](mailto:ecegrad@uwo.ca).
- Structure: 10 courses = 8 technical (5 core + 3 electives) + 2 non-technical. Max 4 courses per term. Project option A: 8 courses + 1 MEng Project; option B: 7 courses + 1 Major Research Project. ([MEng page](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/electrical/graduate/current_students/meng_programs/index.html), OFFICIAL)
- Credits: Western counts courses, not credit hours; credits per course Not found / not published. Project = 2 technical electives (30+ pages); MRP = 3 (45+ pages).
- Co-op: the Faculty MEng Co-op adds one 4-month work term (4 terms total), CAD 125 co-op application fee, CAD 550 co-op tuition plus ancillary fees; paid or not: not stated. ECE is not named on the page, so eligibility is Unverified. [MEng Co-op](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/graduate/future-students/Graduate-Degree-Programs/meng-co-op-program.html) (OFFICIAL)
- Academic standing: cumulative 70% minimum, no grade below 60%. [MEng page](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/electrical/graduate/current_students/meng_programs/index.html) (OFFICIAL)

## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum: 70% average in the last two years of a four-year honours degree or equivalent from an accredited university. [How to apply](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/electrical/graduate/future_students/How-to-apply.html) (OFFICIAL). Western's conversion for the BAIUST 4.00 scale: Not found / not published.
- 4-year bachelor's accepted: Yes (stated basis).
- Background degree: engineering or related is implied; not specified. Prerequisite courses for admission: none listed.
- Documents: personal information, academic history, two references (academic preferred), CV, supplementary questions, statement of purpose, English scores, official transcripts. [How to apply](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/electrical/graduate/future_students/How-to-apply.html) (OFFICIAL)
- GRE, work experience, portfolio, supervisor (not needed for course-based), original vs provisional certificates, average admitted GPA: Not found / not published.
- For comparison: MESc (research) needs 78% and a supervisor.

Do I meet it? Unclear, leaning No. CGPA 3.34 is about 66.64% overall, below the 70% minimum, which is measured on the last two years only with an unpublished conversion. Your last-two-years average could differ. Meeting the minimum does not guarantee admission.
