## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Engineering (MEng), Software Engineering specialization, Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE), Faculty of Engineering, Western University, London, Ontario. [Program page](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/Electrical/graduate/current_students/meng_programs/meng_software.html) (OFFICIAL, no date)
- Degree type: professional master's (MEng). Route: course-based by default; project-based (Regular Project or Major Research Project) only after an approved proposal and change of status. [MEng programs](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/Electrical/graduate/current_students/meng_programs/index.html) (OFFICIAL)
- Campus: London, Ontario. [ECE grad page](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/graduate/future-students/Graduate-Degree-Programs/electrical-computer.html) (OFFICIAL)

## 2. Duration and structure

- Length: 3 terms (1 year); part-time possible. [ECE grad page](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/graduate/future-students/Graduate-Degree-Programs/electrical-computer.html) (OFFICIAL, re-checked)
- Intake: Fall (September) is the clear intake. Winter and Summer shown as "n/a" for MEng deadlines. Winter/Summer start: Unverified. Source above.
- Credits: Western counts courses, not credits. Credit units and credits per course: Not found / not published.
- Course-based: 10 courses = 5 core technical + 3 technical electives + 2 non-technical. Regular Project: 8 courses. Major Research Project: 7 courses. Max 4 courses per term. [Program page](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/Electrical/graduate/current_students/meng_programs/meng_software.html) (OFFICIAL, re-checked)
- Supervisor not needed for admission; projects need an ECE faculty supervisor and are not guaranteed. [Engineering FAQ](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/graduate/future-students/Frequently-Asked-Questions.html) (OFFICIAL, first pass)
- Co-op: optional extra term, only after starting (not at admission). Needs cumulative average of 75% or more, full-time, request by end of term 2 (April 30), \$100 application fee, \$500 co-op module fee, and a co-op work permit for international students. Paid or unpaid: Not found. [Co-op rules](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/graduate/future-students/Graduate-Degree-Programs/meng-programs.html) (OFFICIAL, first pass)
- Standing: cumulative 70% or more each term, no grade below 60%. [MEng programs](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/Electrical/graduate/current_students/meng_programs/index.html) (OFFICIAL)

## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum average: 70% (B- North American average). [ECE grad page](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/graduate/future-students/Graduate-Degree-Programs/electrical-computer.html) (OFFICIAL, re-checked). The central application page does not state 70%; it says SGPS calculates an admission average and program pages list requirements. [Apply](https://www.grad.uwo.ca/admissions/apply.html) (OFFICIAL)
- Background: 4-year bachelor's in Engineering from an accredited university ("a similar program may be considered"). Non-engineering degrees: Not found. Same ECE page.
- 4-year bachelor's accepted: Yes (required). [SGPS international](https://grad.uwo.ca/admissions/international/index.html) (OFFICIAL, first pass)
- Bangladesh CGPA 3.34/4.00 to Western percentage conversion: Not found / not published. Western says it converts non-letter-grade averages. [Engineering FAQ](https://www.eng.uwo.ca/graduate/future-students/Frequently-Asked-Questions.html) (OFFICIAL)
- Prerequisite courses, GRE, work experience: Not found / not published. SOP/CV: SGPS says program-specific documents "may include" a CV and statement of interest; ECE MEng specifics Not found. [Apply](https://www.grad.uwo.ca/admissions/apply.html) (OFFICIAL)
- References: entered online, Western emails each referee a form, due by the application deadline. Number required: Not found. [Apply](https://www.grad.uwo.ca/admissions/apply.html) (OFFICIAL, re-checked)
- Transcripts: unofficial ones are enough to apply; official ones required only after admission; English translation if needed. Original vs provisional certificate: Not found. Same page.
- Average admitted GPA: Not found / not published.

Do I meet it? Unclear, likely No. CGPA 3.34 is about 66.64%, below the 70% minimum, and Western's conversion of a BAIUST 4.0 CGPA is unpublished. A CS or software degree may count only as a "similar program"; ask before paying the fee.
