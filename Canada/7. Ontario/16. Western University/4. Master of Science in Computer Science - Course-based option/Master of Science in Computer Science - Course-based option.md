## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum average: at least 75% (or GPA 3.0/4.0), as determined by the department. [Admission page](https://www.csd.uwo.ca/graduate/future/admission.html) (OFFICIAL, rechecked 2026-11-22)
- Realistic average: the same page says admission with less than 78% is unrealistic, and most accepted MSc students had over 82%. (OFFICIAL)
- Resolution of the 75 / 78 / 70 conflict: these are different levels, not true contradictions. 70% (B-) is the general SGPS floor for all master's programs, based on the last two full-time years, and programs may set higher ([SGPS international page](https://grad.uwo.ca/admissions/international/index.html), OFFICIAL). 75% is the Computer Science department's stated minimum. 78% is the realistic level the department itself describes. The binding figure for this program is 75% minimum (78%+ realistic). The 78%/B+ in a search snippet of the SGPS program page is Unverified because that page could not be opened.
- Background: Honours (4-year) BSc in CS or a related field. Non-CS degrees need very high standing plus CS 2212, CS 3340 and two 3rd/4th-year CS courses (can be taken as a special undergraduate student). (Admission page, OFFICIAL)
- 4-year bachelor's accepted: Yes. 3-year degrees: SGPS says some may not meet the four-year comparability rule ([SGPS](https://grad.uwo.ca/admissions/international/index.html), OFFICIAL).
- Supervisor before applying: not needed. References, SOP, CV, GRE, work experience, portfolio: Not found / not published. Transcripts: current transcripts to apply, final originals if admitted ([FAQ](https://www.csd.uwo.ca/graduate/future/faq.html), OFFICIAL).
- Review: ongoing, decisions normally March or April (FAQ, OFFICIAL).
- Bangladesh grade conversion used by Western: Not found.
