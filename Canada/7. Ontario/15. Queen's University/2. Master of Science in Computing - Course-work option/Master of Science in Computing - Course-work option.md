## 3. Admission requirements

- Honours bachelor's with second-class standing or higher; "International students must have a minimum A standing (first class)." [Apply page](https://www.cs.queensu.ca/apply/graduate.php) (OFFICIAL)
- Grad FAQ cites a CS undergraduate degree with "high upper-second", about 75% ([FAQ](https://cs.queensu.ca/graduate/faq.php), OFFICIAL, search snippet, Unverified; may refer to research MSc).
- Conflict – verify with the university: apply page says first class for international students; FAQ says about 75%; the course-work page lists no GPA. Both are above 66.64%. Canadian equivalent for the Bangladesh scale: Not found.
- Background: CS undergraduate equivalent to Queen's BSc Computing; non-CS with exceptional record may be admitted as qualifying student ([source](https://www.cs.queensu.ca/graduate/admission-types/index.php), OFFICIAL snippet, Unverified). Whether a Bangladesh 4-year bachelor's is accepted: Not found.
- GRE: not required (apply page, OFFICIAL). References: two academic letters, recent, within one year ([SGSPA](https://www.queensu.ca/grad-postdoc/grad-studies/apply), OFFICIAL). SOP: "statement of interest". CV, portfolio, work experience: Not found.
- Supervisor: not required for this pattern (FAQ, Unverified).
- Transcripts: official; translation if not in English; photocopies and website printouts not accepted; degree certificate if transcript lacks degree; final transcripts with degree conferral required before start ([SGSPA](https://www.queensu.ca/grad-postdoc/grad-studies/apply), OFFICIAL). Original vs provisional certificate: Not found.
