## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: MSc Computing, Project pattern ("Project Master's"), School of Computing, Faculty of Arts and Science, Queen's University, Kingston, Ontario. [Project pattern page](https://www.cs.queensu.ca/graduate/msc/patterns/project.php) (OFFICIAL)
- Degree: MSc. Other patterns exist: research and course-work. Route here: six half-courses plus a theoretical or programming project. [MSc page](https://www.cs.queensu.ca/graduate/msc) (OFFICIAL)
- Project course code CISC 898 comes from an archived calendar only ([2021-22 archive](https://www.queensu.ca/academic-calendar/archive/2021-2022/graduate-studies/programs-study/computing/computing-ms/), OFFICIAL, may be outdated). The current calendar page returned no requirement numbers: Unverified.
- Campus: Kingston, Ontario; 557 Goodwin Hall, K7L 2N8 ([contact page](https://www.cs.queensu.ca/apply/graduate.php), OFFICIAL).

## 2. Duration and structure

- Can be finished within 3 full-time terms: Fall and Winter (Sep-Apr) for coursework, then the following Summer term for the project. [Project page](https://www.cs.queensu.ca/graduate/msc/patterns/project.php) (OFFICIAL)
- Project normally takes 2-4 months full-time (about one term), workload comparable to two CISC half-courses (OFFICIAL, same page).
- Intake: normally Fall (September) only ([MSc page](https://www.cs.queensu.ca/graduate/msc), OFFICIAL). The apply page also mentions January 2028 (very few places); see Conflicts.
- Credits: the current pages give half-courses, not credit units. Archived calendar: six 800-level half-courses (CISC 810 excluded; one may be CISC 897) plus CISC 898; at least one course from each of Computer Systems, Theory of Computation, Applications. CISC 897 = 3.00 units ([calendar search](https://queensu.ca/academic-calendar/search/?P=CISC+897), OFFICIAL). Total credits: Unverified.
- Co-op / internship: Not found / not published.
- Part-time option: Not found.
- Pass rule: B- minimum in required courses; failing more than one course normally means withdrawal ([MSc page](https://www.cs.queensu.ca/graduate/msc), OFFICIAL).
- Evaluation: 3-member committee (supervisor, School examiner, Director or delegate) reviews a written report; outcomes Passed / Referred / Failed ([project page](https://www.cs.queensu.ca/graduate/msc/patterns/project.php), OFFICIAL).
- Funding: "the Project MSc is not funded" ([project page](https://www.cs.queensu.ca/graduate/msc/patterns/project.php), OFFICIAL).

## 3. Admission requirements

- Honours bachelor's degree with "second class standing or higher". International applicants need a minimum "A" standing (first class). No GPA or percentage equivalent is given. [Apply page, 2027-28 cycle](https://www.cs.queensu.ca/apply/graduate.php) (OFFICIAL, re-checked 2026-11-22)
- Conflict – verify with the university: [MSc page](https://www.cs.queensu.ca/graduate/msc) says a degree with a computer science concentration and "high upper-second class standing" and does not mention international applicants. The apply page (2027-28 cycle, international "A") is more specific and current, so treat "A" as the working requirement. Third-party [MastersPortal](https://www.mastersportal.com/studies/468988/computing.html) says B- (THIRD-PARTY, low reliability).
- Background: CS undergraduate with calculus, linear algebra, statistics, discrete math, data structures, systems, automata, algorithms, programming languages, software engineering, plus two advanced application courses (AI, databases, graphics). Other disciplines may be admitted as "graduate preparatory" students (up to a year of undergraduate courses). [MSc page](https://www.cs.queensu.ca/graduate/msc) (OFFICIAL)
- 4-year bachelor's: Honours bachelor's required, which implies a 4-year degree. Statement about Bangladesh degrees: Not found.
- GRE: not required. References: 2 academic letters. Also statement of interest, transcripts from all institutions, graduation certificates. Incomplete applications are not considered ([apply page](https://www.cs.queensu.ca/apply/graduate.php), OFFICIAL).
- Transcripts: upload official PDFs; sealed hard copies if upload fails; no photocopies or website printouts; translations needed for non-English; final transcripts sent directly by the issuer after accepting an offer. [SGSPA apply page](https://www.queensu.ca/grad-postdoc/grad-studies/apply) (OFFICIAL)
- CV, portfolio, work experience, supervisor requirement for the project pattern, average admitted GPA: Not found / not published. International applicants are advised to contact a few faculty members.
