## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Engineering (MEng), Electrical and Computer Engineering, course-based with Academic Project (ELEC 898). Professional/coursework master's. [Program page](https://smithengineering.queensu.ca/ece/graduate/meng) (OFFICIAL, checked 2026-11-22)
- Other routes on the same page: pure course-based; course-based with internship (ELEC 895, NOT offered in 2026-27).
- Department: Electrical and Computer Engineering, Smith Engineering. Campus: Kingston, Ontario (Walter Light Hall). Same source.

## 2. Duration and structure

- Duration: two academic terms (fall and winter) plus the project. Intake: fall only. Full-time only stated. [Program page](https://smithengineering.queensu.ca/ece/graduate/meng) (OFFICIAL)
- Fee schedule lists MEng as 3 terms (standard) or 2-term accelerated. [Graduate fees](https://www.queensu.ca/registrar/tuition-fees/graduate) (OFFICIAL)
- Project option: 6 term-length graduate courses + ELEC 898 (counts as 2 courses) + ELEC 891 (seminar) + APSC 801. At least 2 of the 6 must be ECE graduate courses. Up to 2 fourth-year or double-numbered undergraduate courses allowed with approval. (OFFICIAL, program page)
- Total = 8 course equivalents plus ELEC 891 and APSC 801. Queen's states requirements in course equivalents; total credits and credits per course: Not found / not published.
- Co-op/internship: none this year (internship not offered 2026-27). Paid: n/a.
- Fees are charged for an 8-month period, not per course; spring/summer tuition is charged if you do not finish in 2 terms. (OFFICIAL, program page)

## 3. Admission requirements

- GRE: not listed (not required). Work experience: not listed. SOP, portfolio: not listed (Unverified). CV: required (Stage 1 read).
- References: professional programs require 2 professional letters, submitted via the online portal; letters older than 1 year not accepted. Program-specific number: Not found (Unverified for this program). [Apply page](https://www.queensu.ca/grad-postdoc/grad-studies/apply) (OFFICIAL)
- Supervisor required to apply: No.
- Transcripts: official up-to-date transcripts (PDF upload; sealed hard copies if upload fails); final transcripts showing degree conferral sent directly by the issuer before start; no photocopies or web printouts; translation if not English/French.
