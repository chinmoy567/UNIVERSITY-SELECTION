## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Networking Technology (MNET); the fee tables also call it "Master of Information Technology: Network Technology". [Program page](https://carleton.ca/csit/graduate-studies/programs-and-admissions/master-of-networking-technology/) (OFFICIAL)
- Degree: professional, course-based master's; a research essay pathway is mentioned on the [grad program page](https://graduate.carleton.ca/program/information-technology-digital-media-networking-technology-masters/) (OFFICIAL). No thesis. Switching to thesis MASc needs CGPA 10.0 on the 12-point scale, 1 completed term and a supervisor ([program page](https://carleton.ca/csit/graduate-studies/programs-and-admissions/master-of-networking-technology/), OFFICIAL).
- Department: School of Information Technology (CSIT), Faculty of Engineering and Design (OFFICIAL).
- Campus: on campus, 1125 Colonel By Drive, Ottawa, Ontario ([how to apply](https://carleton.ca/csit/graduate-studies/programs-and-admissions/how-to-apply/), OFFICIAL).

## 2. Duration and structure

- 6-term program; the 2-year timeline assumes full-time; part-time also possible. Intakes: September and January (summer not listed). [Program page](https://carleton.ca/csit/graduate-studies/programs-and-admissions/master-of-networking-technology/) (OFFICIAL, re-checked)
- Co-op / internship: Not found / not published (Unverified). Career and job-search help at no extra cost is stated.
- Credits: 2.5 core + 2.5 elective (5000 level) = 5.0 total (the sum is my arithmetic), plus ITEC 5001 seminar (0.0). [Program page](https://carleton.ca/csit/graduate-studies/programs-and-admissions/master-of-networking-technology/) (OFFICIAL)
- Course size: core options are 0.5 credit each, so about 10 courses (arithmetic, Unverified; calendar page unreachable).
- Optional Cybersecurity collaborative specialization: 5.0 credits incl. CYBR 5000 (1.0); limited and competitive (OFFICIAL, same page).

## 3. Admission requirements

- Required background: undergraduate degree in Network Technology, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Engineering or closely related ([how to apply](https://carleton.ca/csit/graduate-studies/programs-and-admissions/how-to-apply/), OFFICIAL).
- Program minimum GPA: Not published on the program page (re-checked). International applicants close to the cut-off may still apply with relevant work experience ([grad program page](https://graduate.carleton.ca/program/information-technology-digital-media-networking-technology-masters/), OFFICIAL).
- University minimum for master's: 4-year honours bachelor's, B+ in the major, B- overall ([minimum requirements](https://graduate.carleton.ca/apply-online/minimum-requirements/), OFFICIAL).
- Bangladesh: a 4-year degree ([B.Sc](http://B.Sc). Eng., B.Eng., [B.Tech](http://B.Tech), 4-year [B.Sc](http://B.Sc). Honours) is accepted. Overall 55-60% = B- minimum; major subject 65-70% = B+ ([Bangladesh page](https://graduate.carleton.ca/international/admission-requirements/bangladesh/), OFFICIAL). Carleton does not publish the BAIUST scale conversion (Unverified).
- Documents: 2 references (academic preferred, institutional email), 1-page statement of intent, CV, transcripts of all post-secondary study, English results. Official documents only if admitted. No portfolio, no supervisor needed ([how to apply](https://carleton.ca/csit/graduate-studies/programs-and-admissions/how-to-apply/), OFFICIAL).
- Prerequisite courses, GRE, work-experience requirement, average admitted GPA: Not found / not published. A 2022 brochure (THIRD-PARTY/older, search snippet only, Unverified) says "B or higher" is expected ([PDF](https://graduate.carleton.ca/wp-content/uploads/CU-FGPA-Engineering-Information-Technology-2022.pdf)).

Do I meet it? Unclear (leaning Yes). CGPA 3.34 (about 66.64%) is above the 55-60% overall level and near or above the 65-70% major level, but the major-subject percentage, BAIUST conversion and field match are not confirmed, and the program publishes no cut-off.
