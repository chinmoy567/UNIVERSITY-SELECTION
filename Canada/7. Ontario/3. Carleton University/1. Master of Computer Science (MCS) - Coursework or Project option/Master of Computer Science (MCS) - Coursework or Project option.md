## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Computer Science (MCS), joint with uOttawa EECS (OCICS), Carleton School of Computer Science, Faculty of Science. [Program page](https://graduate.carleton.ca/program/computer-science-masters/) and [SCS master's page](https://carleton.ca/scs/future-students/graduate-studies/masters-program/) (OFFICIAL, no date)
- Degree: MCS (professional, course-based when non-thesis). Pathways: Thesis, Non-Thesis Project, Non-Thesis Coursework ([news 2025-03-24](https://carleton.ca/scs/2025/new-mcs-non-thesis-pathway/), OFFICIAL).
- Campus and city: Carleton University, Ottawa, Ontario, in person (OFFICIAL, program page).
- Graduate Calendar: [https://calendar.carleton.ca/grad/gradprograms/computerscience/](https://calendar.carleton.ca/grad/gradprograms/computerscience/) (unreachable, Unverified items below).

## 2. Duration and structure

- 2 years, Fall intake only (one intake). Co-op option, work placement up to 8 months in Ottawa high-tech ([program page](https://graduate.carleton.ca/program/computer-science-masters/), OFFICIAL). Co-op paid: Not found / not published.
- Full-time and part-time fee tiers both exist ([fee page](https://carleton.ca/studentaccounts/tuition-fees/fw-gr/f26w27-gr-international), OFFICIAL).
- Total credits (non-thesis): 5.0 ([news](https://carleton.ca/scs/2025/new-mcs-non-thesis-pathway/), OFFICIAL).
- Breakdown: 4.0 credits coursework (min 1.5 OCICS credits across 3 research areas) plus 1.0 credit COMP 5903 Graduate Project or 1.0 more OCICS courses. From 2024-25 calendar summary, THIRD-PARTY snippet, Unverified for 2026-27.
- Credits per course 0.5 (about 10 courses): Unverified.
- Project option needs a supervisor and approved proposal; coursework-only needs none ([news](https://carleton.ca/scs/2025/new-mcs-non-thesis-pathway/), OFFICIAL).

## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum GPA, MCS-specific: Not found (calendar unreachable). Unverified.
- Bangladesh rule (all master's): overall B- (55-60%), major B+ (65-70%) ([Carleton Bangladesh page](https://graduate.carleton.ca/international/admission-requirements/bangladesh/), OFFICIAL, no date).
- 4-year bachelor accepted: Unclear. The page requires bachelor's plus master's for "four-year equivalent", with exceptions ([B.Sc](http://B.Sc). Eng., B.Eng., [B.Tech](http://B.Tech)., 4-year [B.Sc](http://B.Sc). Honours). Verify with [graduate.studies@carleton.ca](mailto:graduate.studies@carleton.ca). Same source.
- Background: honours bachelor's in CS or equivalent; older calendar (2010-11, OUTDATED) asked for 12 CS half-credits and 8 math half-credits ([old calendar](https://www3.carleton.ca/calendars/grad/1011/programs/computerscience_mcs.html)). Current prerequisites Unverified.
- GRE: not required. 2 reference letters, statement of intent, CV, transcripts required; portfolio not mentioned; work experience Not found ([SCS how to apply](https://carleton.ca/scs/how-to-apply-to-graduate-school-in-computer-science/), OFFICIAL).
- Transcripts: PDF uploads at application (max 2 MB, up to 5 files); sealed official paper transcripts after offer; WES ICAP possible ([application FAQ](https://graduate.carleton.ca/application-faq/), OFFICIAL).
- Average admitted GPA: Not found.

Do I meet it? Unclear. CGPA 3.34 (about 66.64%) passes the overall 55-60% minimum and is at the bottom of the major B+ (65-70%) band, but the MCS minimum and the 4-year-degree rule are unconfirmed, and admission is competitive.
