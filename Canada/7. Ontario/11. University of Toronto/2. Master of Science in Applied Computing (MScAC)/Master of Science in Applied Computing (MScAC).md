## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum grade: standing equivalent to at least B+ (U of T 77-79%) in the final year (two terms), or 5 full credits. Only 5-10% are admitted and admitted students usually exceed the minimum. [Apply](https://mscac.utoronto.ca/apply/), [FAQ](https://mscac.utoronto.ca/faq), [Calendar](https://sgs.calendar.utoronto.ca/node/93154) (all OFFICIAL, consistent)
- Background: bachelor's in computer science or a related discipline appropriate to the concentration. 4-year bachelor's expected; 3-year likely insufficient (Unverified). [Calendar](https://sgs.calendar.utoronto.ca/node/93154) (OFFICIAL)
- Prerequisite courses: Not found. Work experience: preferred, not required. [FAQ](https://mscac.utoronto.ca/faq) (OFFICIAL)
- GRE: optional, strongly recommended without a Canadian degree (codes 0982 / 0402); GMAT not accepted. [Apply](https://mscac.utoronto.ca/apply/) (OFFICIAL)
- References: 3 (faculty and/or employers, at least 2 academic if possible). SOP replaced by program-specific essay answers. CV 1-2 pages. Portfolio: not required. Supervisor: not needed. [Apply](https://mscac.utoronto.ca/apply/) (OFFICIAL)
- Transcripts: unofficial scans at application; official copies requested for conditional offers by August 31. Original vs provisional certificate: Not found. [Apply](https://mscac.utoronto.ca/apply/) (OFFICIAL)
- Interview (technical and/or non-technical) is part of the process; decisions rolling. [Apply](https://mscac.utoronto.ca/apply/) (OFFICIAL)
- Average admitted GPA: Not published. BAIUST-to-U of T grade conversion: Not found (Unverified).
