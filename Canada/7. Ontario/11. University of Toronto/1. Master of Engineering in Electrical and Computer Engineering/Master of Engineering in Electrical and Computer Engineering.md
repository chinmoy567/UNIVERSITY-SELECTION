## 2. Duration and structure

- Full-time 1 year (12 months); Extended full-time (EFT) 2 years (16-24 months); Part-time 3 years. [ECE graduates](https://www.ece.utoronto.ca/graduates/) OFFICIAL (re-checked)
- Calendar: FT = 3 sessions, EFT = 6, PT = 9; time limits 3/3/6 years. [Calendar](https://sgs.calendar.utoronto.ca/node/93257) OFFICIAL
- Intakes: Fall (September) or Winter (January); no Summer start. [ECE admissions](https://ece.utoronto.ca/?p=898) OFFICIAL
- Credits: 4.5 FCE = nine half-courses (0.5 FCE each); at least 2.5 FCE (5 half-courses) ECE graduate courses. Project option: 1.5 FCE project + 3.0 FCE coursework. [Calendar](https://sgs.calendar.utoronto.ca/node/93257) OFFICIAL
- Department wording: nine courses, at least six technical, at least five of those ECE. [MEng page](https://www.ece.utoronto.ca/graduates/degree-programs/meng/) OFFICIAL
- Co-op: MEng Co-op = EFT plus one mandatory 8-month paid work term (TEP1702Y; for Fall 2027 starters May-Dec 2028). About CAD 5,000/month average (estimate). Placement not guaranteed, limited spaces, no transfer from regular MEng. [Co-op page](https://gradstudies.engineering.utoronto.ca/master-of-engineering-meng/meng-co-op-program/) OFFICIAL
