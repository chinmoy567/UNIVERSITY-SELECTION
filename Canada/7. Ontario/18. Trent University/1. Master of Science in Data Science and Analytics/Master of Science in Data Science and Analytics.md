## 1. Basics

- Program: MSc Applied Modelling and Quantitative Methods (AMOD), Data Science and Analytics stream, Trent University. [Program page](https://www.trentu.ca/amod/program/data-science-and-analytics-msc) (OFFICIAL)
- Degree: MSc. Route: course-based with a Research Practicum (AMOD-5640Y, external partner) in the final term ([AMOD stream page](https://www.trentu.ca/amod/node/12), OFFICIAL).
- Department: AMOD, School of Graduate Studies.
- Campus: Peterborough (1600 West Bank Drive) and Durham GTA, Oshawa (55 Thornton Rd S) per [program page](https://www.trentu.ca/amod/program/data-science-and-analytics-msc) (OFFICIAL). ## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum: upper second class standing, 77% or better (B+ at Trent) or equivalent, in a joint or single honours BSc or BA ([admission requirements](https://www.trentu.ca/amod/program/admission-requirements), OFFICIAL). Master's programs generally need an equivalent of a 4-year honours bachelor's with B+/77% in the last two years ([international applicants](https://www.trentu.ca/graduatestudies/admissions/how-apply/international-applicants), OFFICIAL).
- Canadian equivalent for Bangladesh scale: Not found (Trent uses WES or U of T CES evaluation).
- 4-year bachelor's: accepted and required (honours-equivalent).
- Prerequisites: none listed for this stream. Course prerequisites: AMOD-5210H one calculus or statistics course; AMOD-5240H advanced statistics and some SAS/SPSS ([course listing](https://www.trentu.ca/amod/program/course-listing), OFFICIAL).
- GRE, SOP, CV, portfolio, work experience: Not found for this stream. Supervisor: thesis stream only.
- References: up to 2 depending on program; referees have 30 days ([how to apply](https://www.trentu.ca/graduatestudies/admissions/how-apply), OFFICIAL). Number for this stream: Unverified.
- Transcripts: unofficial accepted at application, official after an offer; upload within 30 days of the acknowledgement email ([how to apply](https://www.trentu.ca/graduatestudies/admissions/how-apply), OFFICIAL). Graduation certificates required.
- Average admitted GPA: Not published.
- Pre-Master's Pathway (8 months, for near-qualifiers): [page](https://www.trentu.ca/graduatestudies/admissions/pre-masters-pathway) (OFFICIAL; details not read).

Do I meet it? No. CGPA 3.34 is about 66.64%, below the 77% minimum. Options: Pre-Master's Pathway, or ask the school whether an exception or a different percentage conversion applies.
