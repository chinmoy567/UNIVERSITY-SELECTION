## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program page: [Lakehead MSc Computer Science](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/programs/graduate/programs/masters/computer-science) (OFFICIAL)
- Degree: MSc Computer Science. Routes: Course-based (1 year), Project-based (2 years, limited research project), Thesis (2 years) (same page, OFFICIAL)
- Department: Computer Science, Faculty of Graduate Studies (same page, OFFICIAL)
- Campus and city: Thunder Bay, Ontario, and [Barrie STEM Hub](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/about/barrie-stem-hub), Ontario (OFFICIAL)
- Course-based is not suitable for PhD pathway (program page, OFFICIAL)

## 2. Duration and structure

- Length: course-based 1 year, project-based 2 years. Intakes: Fall 2027 (Sept) open; Winter (Jan) open with limited options ([program page](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/programs/graduate/programs/masters/computer-science), OFFICIAL). Graduate intakes Sept, Jan, May for select programs ([dates and fees](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/international/future/dates-and-fees), OFFICIAL)
- Co-op: Project and Thesis only, not Course-based. 8 months (two terms) of co-op employment required; tell the Co-op Advisor 4 months ahead ([program page](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/programs/graduate/programs/masters/computer-science), OFFICIAL). Paid or not: Not found / not published
- Total credits: Unverified. Current official pages give no credit count. Older Lakehead calendar/brochure: 5 full-course equivalents (FCE) = about 10 half-courses (2015-16 [brochure](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/sites/default/files/uploads/35/docs/Brochure_GradCS1.pdf), 2019-20 calendar, OFFICIAL but outdated, seen via search summary). A THIRD-PARTY listing shows 30 credits ([Mastersportal](https://www.mastersportal.com/studies/150067/computer-science.html))

CS 5111 Graduate Seminar = 0.5 credit ([2018-19 catalogue](https://csdc.lakeheadu.ca/Catalog/ViewCatalog.aspx?pageid=viewcatalog&topicgroupid=23286&entitytype=CID&entitycode=Computer+Science+5111), OFFICIAL, old)

- Time limit: finish within 6 terms of continuous registration (program page, OFFICIAL)

## 3. Admission requirements

- Degree: four-year bachelor's or equivalent, at least second-class (B) standing on the last 20 half courses; "B" average in an Honours Computer Science program or equivalent. Four-year bachelor's accepted ([program page](https://www.lakeheadu.ca/programs/graduate/programs/masters/computer-science), OFFICIAL)
- Minimum GPA/percentage: no numeric cutoff published by Lakehead. THIRD-PARTY: 70% ([CanAm Group](https://www.canamgroup.com/program/canada/lakehead-university-barrie-stem-hub/master-of-science-in-computer-science-course-based)); other listings 75% or 3.0/4 (Unverified)
- Conflict – verify with the university: official "B" (not defined numerically) versus third-party 70% / 75%.
- Prerequisites: Computer Science Background Form is mandatory (program page, OFFICIAL)
- References: Course-based 0; Project-based 1-2; Thesis 3 (program page, OFFICIAL)
- Also required: SOP, CV, unofficial final transcript, proof of graduation, proof of English (OFFICIAL). Original vs provisional certificate: Not found
- GRE, work experience, portfolio, supervisor (course-based): not mentioned on the page. Average admitted GPA: Not found / not published
- Admission is not guaranteed; the department must recommend (OFFICIAL)
