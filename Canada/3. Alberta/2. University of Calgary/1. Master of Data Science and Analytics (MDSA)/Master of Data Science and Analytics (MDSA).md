## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Data Science and Analytics (MDSA), course-based (no thesis), professional master's, in person. [Program page](https://grad.ucalgary.ca/future-students/explore-programs/data-science-and-analytics-mdsa-course) OFFICIAL; [Calendar](https://calendar.ucalgary.ca/programs/DSANMDSAN) OFFICIAL
- Faculty/department: Faculty of Science (Science Professional Programs), with Haskayne School of Business and Cumming School of Medicine. Campus: Calgary, Alberta (2500 University Dr NW). OFFICIAL
- Specializations: Business Analytics; Data Science; Health Data Science and Biostatistics; Financial and Energy Markets Data Modelling. OFFICIAL

## 2. Duration and structure

- Designed for 12 months full-time (4 months if moving from the Diploma); continuous, no scheduled break; maximum 6 years. [Program page](https://grad.ucalgary.ca/future-students/explore-programs/data-science-and-analytics-mdsa-course) OFFICIAL (re-checked 2026-11-22)
- Intake: Fall only. Number of terms: Not found / not published.
- Internship: required (professional or research). Paid or unpaid: Not found. Co-op: not mentioned.
- Structure: 4 core + 4 specialization + 1 Integrated Topics + 1 internship = 10 courses. Core: Working with Data and Visualization; Statistical Data Analysis; Statistical Modeling with Data; Big Data Management. OFFICIAL
- Total units and units per course: Not published on the pages that loaded (Program Details tab did not render). Tuition is per 3-unit course, so about 30 units is Unverified (my arithmetic).
- International students apply directly to the Master's; the Certificate/Diploma ladder (24 units laddering credit) is the domestic route. OFFICIAL

## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum GPA: 3.0 on a 4.0 scale over the last two years of full-time study (at least 60 units / 10 full-course equivalents). No percentage cutoff published. [Program page](https://grad.ucalgary.ca/future-students/explore-programs/data-science-and-analytics-mdsa-course) OFFICIAL (re-confirmed); [Science application info](https://science.ucalgary.ca/future-students/professional-programs/application-information) OFFICIAL
- Background: four-year undergraduate degree from a recognised institution (4-year bachelor's accepted). OFFICIAL
- Prerequisites (B or equivalent each): one programming, one statistics, one calculus or linear algebra course. [Calendar admissions](https://calendar.ucalgary.ca/programs/DSANMDSAN/admissions-cMwRI) OFFICIAL
- Documents: unofficial transcripts and degrees with grade scales, proof of prerequisites, GPA calculation worksheet. Original vs provisional certificates: Not found.
- Not required per program page: GRE, reference letters, work samples. SOP, CV, portfolio, supervisor, work experience: not listed (Unverified; confirm). Average admitted GPA: Not found.
- Exceptional-circumstances route exists. OFFICIAL

Do I meet it? Unclear (likely yes on GPA). CGPA 3.34 (about 66.64%) is above 3.0 if the BAIUST 4.0 scale is treated as a 4.0 scale, but the last-two-years GPA via the university worksheet and B-level prerequisite courses must be confirmed.
