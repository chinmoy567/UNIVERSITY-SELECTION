## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Information Security and Privacy (MISP), Faculty of Science, University of Calgary, Calgary, Alberta. [Program page](https://science.ucalgary.ca/information-security) (OFFICIAL); [Calendar entry](https://calendar.ucalgary.ca/programs/MISPMISP) (OFFICIAL); [Grad Studies page](https://grad.ucalgary.ca/future-students/explore-programs/information-security-and-privacy-misp-course) (OFFICIAL)
- Degree: professional master's, course-based, in person; course-based degrees are usually final degrees (Grad Studies page, OFFICIAL).
- Route: course-based plus internship or research/capstone project (6 units) ([Professional programs](https://science.ucalgary.ca/professional-programs), OFFICIAL).
- Departments: Computer Science; Mathematics and Statistics; Faculty of Science; Faculty of Graduate Studies (Calendar entry, OFFICIAL).
- Entry routes: Direct-entry (international students apply directly) and laddered (domestic, two certificates first; [LADMISP](https://calendar.ucalgary.ca/programs/LADMISP), OFFICIAL).

## 2. Duration and structure

- Direct-entry: full-time, continuous, about 12 months expected, 4 years maximum (Grad Studies page and Calendar entry, OFFICIAL). Laddered: about 2 years, full- or part-time.
- Intake: Fall only ([Application information](https://science.ucalgary.ca/future-students/professional-programs/application-information), OFFICIAL). Number of terms: Not found / not published.
- Co-op: none. Optional competitive professional internship in industry for the final 6 units; paid status Not found (Professional programs, OFFICIAL).
- Total: 30 units = 8 courses (24 units) plus internship or capstone (6 units) (Professional programs, OFFICIAL; Calendar says Faculty of Science requires 30 units).
- Structure: Graduate Certificate in Network Security (12) plus Graduate Certificate in Data Privacy (12) per the [program page](https://science.ucalgary.ca/information-security); the Grad Studies page says 2 of 3 certificates (Software Security also). Conflict – verify with the university.
- Credits per course: 3 units (Unverified; tuition is quoted per 3-unit course). Units are UCalgary credit units.

## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum GPA: 3.00 on a 4-point scale or equivalent, on the last 60 units (20 half-course equivalents) of a 4-year bachelor's ([Application information](https://science.ucalgary.ca/future-students/professional-programs/application-information), OFFICIAL). Bangladesh/BAIUST conversion: Not found / not published (GPA Calculation Worksheet required).
- Background: 4-year bachelor's accepted. Prerequisites: programming, algorithms and data structures, and operating systems or computer networks; Proof of Prerequisite Completion form required (OFFICIAL).
- GRE: not required. Reference letters: not required. Work experience: none stated. SOP / CV / portfolio: Not found. Supervisor: not applicable.
- Documents: unofficial transcripts and degrees with grade scales, proof of prerequisites, GPA Calculation Worksheet. Original vs provisional certificates: Not found.
