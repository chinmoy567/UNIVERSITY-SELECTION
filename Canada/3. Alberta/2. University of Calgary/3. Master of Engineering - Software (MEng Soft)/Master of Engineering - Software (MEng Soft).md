## Full details (original notes)

## 1. Basics

- Program: Master of Engineering - Software (MEng Soft), specialization in Software Engineering within Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Calgary. [Grad program page](https://grad.ucalgary.ca/future-students/explore-programs/electrical-and-computer-engineering-meng-software-course) (OFFICIAL); [Schulich page](https://schulich.ucalgary.ca/electrical-computer/programs/graduate/master-engineering-course-based-software) (OFFICIAL)
- Degree: professional MEng, course-based (no thesis, no supervisor needed) (grad program page, OFFICIAL)
- Department: Electrical and Software Engineering, Schulich School of Engineering (OFFICIAL)
- Campus: Calgary, Alberta (ENC 205, 2500 University Drive NW) (OFFICIAL)

## 2. Duration and structure

- Two years full-time; can be accelerated to 8 months (Fall start) or 12 months (Spring start) ([grad page](https://grad.ucalgary.ca/future-students/explore-programs/electrical-and-computer-engineering-meng-software-course), OFFICIAL). Schulich page says "two years or less".
- Intakes: page says Fall and Winter intake, but its deadline table also lists May 1 (Spring), Sep 1 (Fall), Jan 1 (Winter) admission. Conflict – verify with the university.
- Internship: optional paid 8-16 month full-time internship, limited spots, extends program to 3 years (OFFICIAL). No co-op mentioned.
- Structure: 10-13 courses = 10 core advanced courses (2 of them a team design project) + 3 foundation courses if no software background; up to 2 general engineering courses may count (OFFICIAL).
- Foundation: 9 units (3 courses) can be exempted for 4-year Software Engineering degree holders ([Calendar admissions](https://calendar.ucalgary.ca/programs/ENECMENG/admissions-cMwRI), OFFICIAL).
- Total credits: not published on fetched pages. Tuition is per 3-unit course, so 10 courses = about 30 units (arithmetic, Unverified). Course requirement PDFs on [schulich.ucalgary.ca](http://schulich.ucalgary.ca) returned 404.

## 3. Admission requirements

- Minimum GPA: 3.0/4.0 over the last two years of full-time undergraduate study (at least 10 full-course equivalents or 60 units) ([grad page](https://grad.ucalgary.ca/future-students/explore-programs/electrical-and-computer-engineering-meng-software-course), OFFICIAL). Percentage equivalent: Not found / not published.
- Required degree: four-year bachelor's in engineering from a recognized institution; other disciplines may be considered with extra undergraduate courses ([Calendar](https://calendar.ucalgary.ca/programs/ENECMENG/admissions-cMwRI), OFFICIAL). A 4-year bachelor's is accepted.
- GRE: none. References, SOP/CV: none listed for this program (general graduate page says these are program-dependent; confirm in the portal, Unverified). Supervisor: not required. Work experience: not required.
- Documents: unofficial transcripts (one PDF per institution) uploaded after applying; provisional and degree certificates where available; official transcripts sent directly by the institution only after an offer ([FGS apply page](https://grad.ucalgary.ca/future-students/apply), OFFICIAL).
- Average admitted GPA: Not found / not published.

Do I meet it? Unclear (leaning Yes if the degree is engineering). CGPA 3.34 on the BAIUST scale (about 66.64%) is not converted by UCalgary on any page I found; the 3.0/4.0 minimum is applied to the Canadian-equivalent GPA of the last two years. The degree must be in engineering.
